**If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (U.S.), or call 911 in an emergency.** The Art of Connect is not a crisis or emergency service.

- **Not a healthcare provider.** The Art of Connect does not provide medical, psychological or clinical treatment. In Life content — including assessments, exercises and journaling — is for personal growth and reflection, not diagnosis or treatment.
- **Independent professionals.** Counselors and coaches are independent professionals. Licensed clinicians are clearly identified as licensed; coaches and mentors are identified as nonclinical and do not provide therapy.
- **Your choice of professional.** You are responsible for choosing a professional who meets your needs. We verify licenses for licensed professionals but do not supervise the services they provide.
- **Confidentiality.** Your In Life activity is private and separate from your other profiles. Information is shared with a professional only with your consent.
