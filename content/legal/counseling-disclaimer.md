This disclaimer explains the nature of the personal-growth and professional services available through **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us"), especially in **The Art of Becoming — In Life**. It applies to every member and is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service).

## 1. Not an emergency service

The Art of Connect is **not an emergency service**. We do not monitor journals, messages, assessments or appointments in real time.

**If you are in crisis or may harm yourself or someone else, call 911 (or your local emergency number) right away. In the United States you can also call or text 988 to reach the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.**

## 2. The Art of Connect does not provide clinical treatment

The Art of Connect is a membership platform for adults aged **40 and older**. The company itself does **not diagnose, treat or provide clinical care** for any mental-health or medical condition. Nothing on the platform replaces care from a qualified licensed professional.

## 3. Different kinds of support, clearly distinguished

You may encounter four different kinds of support on the platform. They are not the same:

- **Personal development and educational content.** The Art of Becoming offers guided reflection, a private journal, emotional-intelligence exercises and educational content. These are self-reflection tools for personal growth.
- **Coaching.** Coaches help you set and pursue goals in areas such as relationships, career or wellbeing. **Coaching is not therapy.** Coaches do not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions.
- **Mentorship.** Mentors in The Professional Society share their professional experience. Mentorship is not legal, financial, tax or investment advice unless a mentor holds the relevant license and clearly says they are acting in that capacity.
- **Licensed mental-health services.** Licensed professionals, such as licensed counselors, therapists, social workers or psychologists, may offer services only where they are licensed to practice and only within the scope of their license. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: whether licensed clinical services and telehealth sessions will be offered through the platform at launch.]**

Each professional's profile states the category they were approved under. Please read it before booking.

## 4. Assessments and journals are for self-reflection

Compatibility, readiness, personality and emotional-intelligence assessments, and the private journal, are **self-reflection tools**. They:

- are **not** psychological tests or clinical evaluations;
- do **not** diagnose any condition;
- do **not** predict the success of a relationship, partnership or business venture.

Your private journal entries and assessment answers stay private to you. They are never shared with other members, professionals or our staff unless you choose to share them, and they are never used for advertising or to train artificial-intelligence models. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## 5. Professionals are independent

Counselors, coaches and mentors on the platform are **independent professionals**, not employees or agents of The Art of Connect. They are responsible for their own services, advice, records and professional conduct.

When a professional shows a **verified** badge, our staff completed identity and credential checks, such as a license lookup and disciplinary-record review, at the time of verification. Verification:

- is a point-in-time check of the information available to us;
- does **not** guarantee a professional's skill, judgment, conduct or results;
- does **not** mean The Art of Connect supervises or endorses their services.

You remain responsible for choosing a professional who suits your needs. Licensing boards in your state can confirm a license's current status.

## 6. No guaranteed outcomes

Personal growth is personal. The Art of Connect, its content and the professionals on it **cannot guarantee** any outcome, including emotional wellbeing, relationship success, reconciliation, marriage, career or business results.

## 7. Consult qualified professionals

Talk to a qualified professional before making significant decisions about your health, wellbeing, relationships, finances or legal matters. If you have a medical or mental-health condition, consult your physician or a licensed mental-health professional. Do not stop or change any treatment based on content from the platform.

## 8. Privacy of what you share with professionals

What you choose to share with a professional is governed by our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and by that professional's own obligations. The Art of Connect is **not represented as HIPAA compliant**, and platform messaging should not be relied on as a clinical record. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: HIPAA applicability and health-information privacy obligations before any clinical services are enabled.]**

## 9. Reporting concerns

If a professional behaves inappropriately, misrepresents their credentials or makes you feel unsafe, report it through the platform's reporting tools or by email to **info@theartofconnect.com**. See our [Member Safety and Reporting Policy](/legal/safety-policy).
