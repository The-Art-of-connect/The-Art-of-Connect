This Professional Agreement applies to counselors, coaches, mentors and other professionals ("Professionals") who offer services through **The Art of Connect, LLC**.

## Independent professionals
Professionals are independent and are solely responsible for the services they provide, their professional judgment, and compliance with laws and licensing rules that apply to them. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment, partnership or agency relationship.

## Eligibility and verification
- Professionals must meet our minimum professional age and credential requirements.
- **Licensed mental health professionals** must hold an active, unrestricted license in good standing in each state where they serve clients, and must keep their license current. We verify licenses with the issuing board and check for disciplinary actions before approval and periodically afterward.
- **Coaches, mentors and other nonclinical professionals** must not represent their services as therapy, counseling or clinical treatment, and are clearly labeled as nonclinical.
