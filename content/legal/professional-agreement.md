This Professional Provider Agreement ("Agreement") is between **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us") and the counselor, coach, mentor or other professional ("Provider," "you") who applies to offer services to members through theartofconnect.com and its related experiences: **The Professional Society — In Business**, **The Art of Becoming — In Life** and **The Personal Society — In Love**.

This Agreement works alongside our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service), [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards). If this Agreement conflicts with those documents on a matter specific to Providers, this Agreement controls.

## 1. Who this Agreement covers

This Agreement applies to every professional who applies for, or holds, a Provider account, including:

- **Licensed mental-health professionals**, such as licensed professional counselors, licensed marriage and family therapists, licensed clinical social workers, psychologists and similar licensed clinicians.
- **Coaches**, including life, relationship, executive, career and wellness coaches, whether or not they hold a private certification.
- **Mentors**, including experienced business leaders and professionals who offer guidance through The Professional Society.
- **Other non-clinical professionals** offering educational, personal-development or advisory services that we approve.

The category you apply under determines which verification steps apply to you and how your profile is described to members.

## 2. Licensed services versus coaching, mentorship and personal development

The Art of Connect draws a clear line between these kinds of services, and you must respect it in everything you say and do on the platform:

- **Licensed mental-health services** are the assessment, diagnosis or treatment of mental-health conditions by a professional who holds a current license to provide those services in the client's jurisdiction.
- **Coaching** helps a person set and pursue goals. **Coaching is not therapy.** Coaches must not diagnose, treat or claim to treat any mental-health condition, and must refer members to licensed professionals when a need falls outside coaching.
- **Mentorship** shares professional experience and perspective. It is not legal, financial, tax, investment or clinical advice unless the mentor is separately licensed and clearly states that they are providing such advice in that capacity.
- **Educational content and personal development**, including the guided reflection, journals and assessments in **The Art of Becoming — In Life**, are self-reflection tools. They are not clinical services.

You must describe your services accurately within the category for which you were approved.

## 3. Not an emergency service; no clinical treatment by the platform

The Art of Connect is **not an emergency service** and does not monitor messages, journals or appointments in real time. The Art of Connect itself does **not provide clinical treatment**. If a member indicates a risk of harm to themselves or others, you must follow your own professional, ethical and legal obligations, which may include directing the member to call **911** (or local emergency services) or the **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** (call or text 988 in the United States).

## 4. Independent contractor relationship

- You are an **independent professional**. You are not an employee, agent, partner or joint venturer of The Art of Connect.
- You control how you deliver your services, including your methods, schedule (subject to the platform's booking tools) and professional judgment.
- You are solely responsible for your own taxes, business licenses, registrations, insurance, record-keeping and compliance with laws and professional rules that apply to you.
- You may not represent that The Art of Connect employs, supervises, endorses or guarantees your services. A "verified" badge means only that we completed the checks described in Section 5 at the time they were performed.

## 5. Credential and identity verification

Before your profile is published, our staff complete a verification checklist. **Approval is refused until every applicable item is complete.** The checklist includes:

1. **Identity** — confirmation that you are who you say you are, which may use a third-party identity verification provider. We do not store copies of government ID documents ourselves.
2. **License lookup** — for licensed professionals, verification of your license number, type, status and expiration with the issuing state board or official registry.
3. **Disciplinary check** — review of available public records of disciplinary action, restrictions or sanctions.
4. **Insurance** — confirmation of professional liability insurance where required under Section 7.
5. **References** — confirmation of professional references where we request them.

You agree that the information you provide is true, complete and current, and you authorize us to verify it with the relevant boards, registries and references. Verification is a point-in-time check. It is not a guarantee of your skill, conduct or the outcome of any service.

## 12. Honest representation; no guarantees

You must not:

- misrepresent your identity, licenses, credentials, education, experience, affiliations or reviews;
- use titles such as "therapist," "counselor," "psychologist" or "licensed" unless you are legally entitled to use them in the member's jurisdiction;
- **guarantee outcomes**, including relationship success, marriage, reconciliation, business results, income or recovery from any condition;
- create fake reviews or ask members for reviews in exchange for discounts or other benefits.
