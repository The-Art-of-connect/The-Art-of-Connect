The Art of Connect is a community of adults who value respect, honesty and intention. These standards apply in every experience.

## Be genuine
- Use your real identity, recent photos and accurate information.
- One account per person. No impersonation.

## Be respectful
- No harassment, hate speech, threats, or discriminatory treatment.
- Respect boundaries. "No" — or no reply — is an answer.
- No sexually explicit content or unsolicited explicit messages.

## Be safe and honest
- **Never ask other members for money**, investments, gift cards or financial details.
- No scams, spam, solicitation or pyramid schemes. In Business introductions are for genuine professional relationships.
- Do not share another member's private information without permission.

## Respect privacy and confidentiality
- What members share with you in confidence stays confidential.
- Do not record or screenshot private conversations to share publicly.

## Professionals
Counselors, coaches and mentors must also follow the [Professional Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement) and their own professional ethics codes.

## Reporting and enforcement
Report concerns from **Safety** in your account or at info@theartofconnect.com. Reports are confidential. We may warn, restrict, suspend or remove members, and we cooperate with law enforcement where required.
