The Art of Connect, LLC ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us") is a private community for adults aged 21 and older. Our three experiences — **The Professional Society** (In Business), **The Art of Becoming** (In Life) and **The Personal Society** (In Love) — depend on one thing above all: that people can trust the person on the other side of the conversation. These Community Standards describe what we expect from every member, couple and professional provider. They form part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service), and breaking them can lead to the removal of content, restricted features, suspension or permanent closure of an account.

## 1. Who these standards apply to
These standards apply to everyone who uses The Art of Connect: individual members, couples, professional providers (counselors, coaches, mentors and others) and our own team. They apply to profiles, messages, introductions, events, appointments, reviews, shared content and any contact that begins on the platform, including contact that continues by phone, email, social media or in person.

## 2. Be who you say you are
- **One person, one account.** Your account is personal to you. Do not share it, sell it or let anyone else use it. Couples each hold their own individual account (see our [Couples Membership Agreement](/legal/couples-agreement)).
- **No fake profiles or impersonation.** Do not pretend to be someone else, use another person's photographs, misstate your age, relationship status, profession, credentials or intentions, or create an account on behalf of another person.
- **Accurate photographs.** Photographs should be reasonably recent and should show you. Do not use filters or edits that materially change your appearance.
- **Honest intentions.** In The Personal Society, be truthful about whether you are single, separated or in a relationship. In The Professional Society, be truthful about your business, title and experience.
- **Verification.** We may ask you to verify your identity and age through our third-party provider. Attempting to defeat or misuse verification is a serious violation.

## 3. Respect every member
- **No harassment.** Do not insult, demean, intimidate, bully or repeatedly contact someone who has not responded or has asked you to stop.
- **No stalking or tracking.** Do not monitor, follow or locate another member, attempt to discover where they live or work, or contact them through other channels after they have declined contact or blocked you.
- **No threats or violence.** Threats of harm — to a person, their family, their property, their reputation or their business — are never acceptable and may be reported to law enforcement.
- **No discrimination or hate.** Do not attack or exclude anyone on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, veteran status or any other protected characteristic. Members may state genuine personal preferences for romantic introductions; expressing those preferences with contempt or hostility toward a group is not allowed.
- **Graciousness when declining.** Everyone is free to decline an introduction, a meeting or a conversation, without giving a reason. Accept "no" respectfully.

## 4. Consent and sexual conduct
- **No unwanted sexual communications.** Do not send sexual messages, comments or requests to anyone who has not clearly welcomed them, and never in The Professional Society or The Art of Becoming.
- **No explicit imagery.** Do not send or post nude, sexually explicit or graphic images. This applies even between consenting members while using our services.
- **No sexual solicitation or commercial sex.** Offering, requesting or advertising sexual services is prohibited.
- **Consent is ongoing.** Agreeing to a conversation, a video call or a meeting is not agreement to anything more, and consent can be withdrawn at any time.

## 5. Protect other people's privacy
- **Do not share another member's private information.** This includes their full name, contact details, address, workplace, photographs, messages, financial details, health information, relationship history and anything they share in confidence — whether on or off the platform — without their clear permission.
- **No screenshots for exposure.** Do not record, screenshot or publish another member's profile or messages to embarrass, pressure or expose them.
- **Couples' privacy.** A partner's private journal entries, reflections and assessment results belong to that partner. Do not attempt to access a partner's account.
- **Professional confidentiality.** Professional providers must follow the confidentiality obligations in the [Professional Provider Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement) and their own professional rules.

## 6. Money, scams and solicitation
Romance scams and financial fraud cause real harm, and they target adults of every age. Therefore:
- **Never ask another member for money,** gifts, gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, loans, travel costs, medical costs or help with a "temporary" financial emergency.
- **No investment pitches or unsolicited business solicitation.** Do not pitch investments, trading schemes, cryptocurrency, multi-level marketing or "opportunities" to members, including in The Professional Society, unless the other member has clearly asked to discuss a business proposal. Even then, see our [Business Networking Disclaimer](/legal/business-networking-disclaimer).
- **No spam or advertising.** Do not use messaging to advertise products or services. Professional providers may promote their services only through the tools we provide for that purpose.
- **Report it.** If anyone asks you for money or financial information, stop contact and report them. We take these reports seriously and act quickly.

## 7. Safe and lawful use
- Do not use The Art of Connect for any unlawful purpose, or to promote violence, self-harm, illegal drugs or weapons.
- Do not upload viruses, scrape data, create automated accounts or attempt to access accounts, systems or information you are not authorized to access. Security researchers should follow our [Security and Responsible Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](/legal/security-disclosure).
- Do not post content that infringes someone else's copyright, trademark or other rights.
- Do not misrepresent professional credentials or offer licensed services you are not authorized to provide.

## 8. Professional conduct in The Professional Society and The Art of Becoming
- Keep introductions and conversations professional and courteous.
- Respect the time of mentors, coaches and peers. Do not use introductions to recruit for unrelated ventures without permission.
- Professional providers must stay within their scope of practice, never guarantee outcomes, and never pursue a romantic or sexual relationship with a client.

## 9. Reporting and blocking
- **Block** anyone at any time from the member area. Blocking takes effect immediately, is private (we do not notify the other person) and prevents them from viewing your profile or contacting you through the platform.
- **Report** a profile, message, appointment or behavior from the member area or by emailing info@theartofconnect.com. You do not need to be certain a rule was broken to report a concern.
- **We review reports** confidentially. We do not tell the reported person who made the report, unless required by law.
- **False or malicious reports** made to harass another member are themselves a violation of these standards.

See our [Member Safety and Reporting Policy](/legal/safety-policy) for details.

## 10. What happens when standards are broken
Depending on how serious the conduct is, whether it has happened before and the risk to others, we may:
- remove content or a profile photograph;
- issue a warning;
- limit features such as messaging or introductions;
- temporarily suspend an account while we investigate;
- permanently close an account and refuse future registrations;
- report conduct to law enforcement or a professional licensing board where appropriate or required.

We may act immediately, without advance notice, where we believe someone is at risk. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether any membership fees are refunded when an account is closed for a violation — must align with the Cancellation and Refund Policy and applicable consumer law.]**

## 11. Appeals
If your account is restricted, suspended or closed, you may ask us to review the decision by writing to info@theartofconnect.com with your account email and any information you would like us to consider. A team member who was not involved in the original decision will review your appeal where practical. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: appeal timeframe — how long members have to appeal and how quickly we respond.]** Appeals are not available where we are legally prohibited from reversing a decision or where a member poses a credible safety risk.

## 12. Emergencies
The Art of Connect is **not an emergency service** and does not monitor conversations in real time. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call **911** (or your local emergency number). If you are thinking about suicide or are in emotional crisis, call or text **988** to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the United States.
