This Privacy Policy explains how **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us") handles personal information when you visit theartofconnect.com, create an account, or use our membership experiences: **The Professional Society — In Business**, **The Art of Becoming — In Life**, and **The Personal Society — In Love**. Our services are offered only to adults aged 40 and older.

We wrote this policy to describe what our platform actually does. If a practice is not yet decided, we say so rather than guess.

## 1. Who we are and how to contact us

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us
- **Account details:** your first name or display name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only in hashed form; we never see your password.
- **Date of birth:** collected at registration to confirm you are 40 or older. It is stored in a private record that only you can read. Our staff, including the owner, see only whether your account meets the age requirement, never the date itself.
- **Membership choices:** the experiences, membership package, billing interval and any add-ons you select.
- **Profile and experience content:** information you choose to add within each experience, such as professional interests in In Business or relationship preferences in In Love.
- **Private journals and assessments:** reflections, journal entries and assessment answers you create, particularly in In Life and In Love.
- **Communications:** messages you exchange with other members or professionals through the platform, and messages you send to us.
- **Professional applications:** for counselors, coaches and mentors, your legal name, professional type, license or credential details, specialties and related information you submit for verification.
- **Reports and safety information:** reports you file about another member, and blocks you place.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Security information:** when you sign in, reset a password or submit protected forms, we record the event type, time and a **one-way hash** of your IP address together with a shortened browser description. We do not store raw IP addresses in these logs, and logs never contain passwords, journal entries or message contents.
- **Essential cookies:** used to keep you signed in and secure. See our [Cookie and Tracking Policy](/legal/cookie-policy).
- **Campaign attribution:** if you arrive from a marketing link or a member's referral link, we store the campaign source and referral code in a first-party cookie so we can credit the referral.

### 2.3 Information from service providers
- **Payments:** Stripe tells us whether a payment succeeded, failed or was refunded, along with amounts and dates. We never receive or store your full card number.
- **Identity verification:** if identity verification is required, Stripe Identity tells us the outcome (for example, verified or not verified). We do not receive or store images of your government ID or your selfie.
- **Bot protection:** Cloudflare Turnstile tells us whether a form submission passed a bot check.

## 3. Sensitive information
Some information you share is especially personal: private journals, personal and relationship assessments, romantic preferences, private communications, identity verification results, financial and billing information, professional credentials, and account security information. We protect it as follows:
- **Private journals, assessments and confidential communications are not visible to our staff, including the owner.** Our database rules restrict them to you (and, for communications, the other participants).
- We **do not sell** sensitive personal information.
- We **do not use** private journals, assessments or confidential communications **for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.**
- We **never include** journal entries, assessment answers, dates of birth or message contents **in emails**.

## 4. How we use information
We use personal information to:
1. Create and secure your account, including verifying that you are 40 or older.
2. Provide the experiences you choose and the features within them.
3. Process membership payments, renewals, upgrades, cancellations and refunds through Stripe.
4. Send transactional emails, such as account, membership, payment and safety notices.
5. Keep the community safe: detect fake profiles, investigate reports, prevent fraud and enforce our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards).
6. Verify professional credentials before a professional is listed.
7. Protect our systems, including bot protection and security monitoring.
8. Comply with law and respond to lawful requests.
9. Understand, in aggregate, how memberships are used so we can improve the service.

We send marketing emails only where you have opted in, and marketing consent is always separate from accepting our agreements. You can withdraw marketing consent at any time.

## 5. Keeping your experiences separate
In Business, In Life and In Love are kept separate. Information you create in one experience is not shared with members of another experience unless you choose to share it.

**Couples** each have their own individual account, password and private space. Your partner cannot read your private journal or individual assessments. You decide what, if anything, is shared in your shared relationship space, and you can change that choice.

## 6. How we share information
We share personal information only as described here:
- **With other members, as you direct:** for example, profile details you choose to show to potential matches or professional connections.
- **With professionals you choose to work with:** only the information you choose to share with them.
- **With our service providers**, who process information on our behalf under contract:
  - **Supabase** — database and authentication hosting.
  - **Netlify** — website hosting and delivery.
  - **Stripe** — payment processing and, where used, **Stripe Identity** for identity and age verification.
  - **Resend** — delivery of transactional email.
  - **Cloudflare** — Turnstile bot protection on our forms.

## 7. Who at The Art of Connect can see your information
Staff access is limited by role (for example, support, moderation, finance), and every staff account must use two-factor authentication. Staff actions on sensitive records are recorded in audit logs. Owner access does not include private journals, assessments or confidential communications.

## 8. How we protect information
- Database row-level security so members can read only their own private records.
- Encryption in transit (HTTPS) across our website and service providers.
- Mandatory two-factor authentication for staff.
- Bot protection and rate limiting on sign-in, registration, password recovery and other public forms.
- Security logs that use hashed IP addresses and exclude passwords and private content.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident affecting your personal information, we will notify you and regulators as required by law. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: state breach-notification timelines and content requirements]**

- Request a copy of your personal information.

Send requests to **info@theartofconnect.com**. We will verify your identity before acting on a request and will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
