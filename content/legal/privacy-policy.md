This Privacy Policy explains how **The Art of Connect, LLC** collects, uses and protects personal information.

## Information we collect
- **Account details:** name, email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider).
- **Date of birth:** used only to confirm you meet our minimum age. It is kept private to you and is **not** shown on your profile or to our staff.
- **Identity verification outcome:** if you verify your identity, our provider (Stripe Identity) handles your government ID and selfie. **We do not store your ID document or selfie** — we receive only whether verification succeeded and whether you meet our age requirement.
- **Profile and experience information** you choose to provide in In Business, In Life or In Love. Each experience has a separate profile.
- **Private content** such as journal entries and private reflections, which are visible **only to you** — not to other members, partners or our staff.
- **Payments:** processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
- **Safety information:** reports you make or that are made about you, and blocks.
- **Usage and campaign data:** pages visited and the campaign or referral link that brought you to us, recorded with first-party cookies. We do not use third-party advertising trackers [UPDATE IF MARKETING PIXELS ARE ADDED WITH CONSENT].

## How we use information
To provide and secure the services, confirm eligibility, make introductions you request, process payments, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to support requests, and (with your consent) send marketing messages.

## Sharing
We share information with service providers that operate the platform (hosting, database, payments, email, identity verification) under contracts that protect it, when you ask us to (for example, consent-based referrals to a professional), and where required by law. **We do not sell personal information.**

## Separation of experiences
Your In Life activity, journal and any professional relationships are not shown on your In Love or In Business profiles. Couples' individual reflections are never shared with a partner automatically.
