This Membership Agreement ("Agreement") applies when you purchase a paid membership from **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("we," "us"). It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and works together with our [Subscription and Automatic Renewal Policy](/legal/subscription-auto-renewal) and [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).

## 1. Membership eligibility
- You must be **40 or older** (or meet any higher minimum age we publish) and hold an account in good standing.
- We may require identity and age verification through an approved third-party provider before some features are available, for example matchmaking introductions.
- Memberships are for personal use by the account holder only.

## 2. Membership options
The memberships available, their names, what each includes and their prices are shown on our Memberships page and at checkout. They currently fall into these groups:

- **Individual experiences:** The Professional Society (In Business), The Art of Becoming (In Life) and The Personal Society (In Love, for singles).
- **Couples Membership (In Love):** for two partners. Each partner has a **separate individual account**, gives their own consent, and controls what personal information is shared with the other. See the [Couples Membership Agreement](/legal/couples-agreement).
- **Bundles:** two experiences together (for example Dual Society — In Business and In Love; Business + Life; Life + Love) or all three (Complete Society), at a bundled rate.
- **Exclusive memberships:** Executive Private and Elite Concierge, which include all three experiences and additional benefits described at checkout. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: final concierge benefits and their limits before Elite Concierge is sold]**

Professional provider subscriptions are governed by the [Professional Provider Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement), not this Agreement.

## 3. One account, every device
Your membership is one account that you can use on your phone, tablet and computer. **There are no additional device charges.** Sharing your login with another person is not permitted.

## 4. Price and what you pay
- You pay the price shown at checkout for the membership and billing period you choose, plus any applicable taxes. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: sales tax / digital services tax obligations by state]**
- An **onboarding fee** applies only if it is clearly shown at checkout before you pay. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether any onboarding fee applies, and to which memberships]**
- Annual memberships may be offered at a reduced price compared with paying monthly. The annual price is shown at checkout.
- Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

## 5. Billing periods and automatic renewal
Memberships are billed **monthly or annually**, as you choose, and **renew automatically** at the end of each period until you cancel. Before you pay, checkout shows the price, the billing frequency, when you will next be charged and how to cancel, and asks for your affirmative consent. Full details are in the [Subscription and Automatic Renewal Policy](/legal/subscription-auto-renewal).

## 6. Promotional codes and offers
- Promotional codes apply only as described with the offer. Unless the offer says otherwise, a code applies to your **first billing period only**, after which the regular price applies.
- Codes may have expiry dates, usage limits or apply only to certain memberships. They have no cash value and cannot be combined unless stated.
- We may withdraw a code that was issued in error or used in violation of its terms.

## 7. Upgrades and downgrades
- You can change your membership (for example, add an experience, move to a bundle, or switch between monthly and annual billing) from **My membership**.
- Changes take effect immediately. Our payment processor calculates a **prorated** charge or credit for the rest of the current billing period, which appears on your next invoice.
- When you remove an experience, your access to it ends when the change takes effect. Your private journal and other personal records are kept in your account unless you delete them, as described in our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).

## 8. Failed payments
If a renewal payment fails, our payment processor will try again over a short period and we will notify you by email so you can update your payment method. If payment still cannot be collected, we may **suspend paid features** until the balance is paid, or end the membership. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: length of grace period before suspension]**

## 9. Cancellation
You can cancel online at any time from **My membership**, without calling or emailing us. Cancellation stops future renewals. Your access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: any cancellation fee — none assumed]** Refunds are covered by the [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).

## 10. Price changes
We may change membership prices in the future. We will give you advance notice by email before a new price applies to your renewal, and you can cancel before it takes effect. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: advance notice period for price changes — at least 30 days recommended for attorney review]**

## 11. Chargebacks and payment disputes
If you believe you were charged in error, please contact us first at info@theartofconnect.com; most issues can be resolved quickly. If you file a chargeback with your bank, we may **suspend your account while the dispute is open** and provide your transaction records to the payment processor. Filing a chargeback does not remove your right to dispute a charge under applicable law.

## 12. Membership features and safety
Membership gives access to features; it does not guarantee introductions, matches, business opportunities or results. We may change features over time and will tell you about material reductions in what your membership includes. We may suspend or end a membership for violations of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) or [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards) under our [Member Safety and Reporting Policy](/legal/safety-policy). **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether any refund applies when a membership is ended for a violation]**

**Nothing in this Agreement limits rights you have under applicable law**, including any cooling-off, cancellation or refund rights provided by state dating-service or consumer-protection laws. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: state dating-service contract laws (cancellation windows, required notices) for In Love memberships]**
