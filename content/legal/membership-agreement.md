This Membership Agreement applies to paid memberships of **The Art of Connect, LLC**.

## Memberships
- **Signature Membership:** any one experience.
- **Premier Membership:** any two experiences at a bundled rate.
- **Elite Membership:** all three experiences at our best bundled rate.
- **Couples upgrade (In Love):** adds a second, secure login for your partner.
Current prices, discounts and benefits are shown on our [Memberships page](/membership) at the time of purchase.

## One account, one subscription
All experiences you choose are billed together as one subscription. There are no additional fees for using the services on more than one device.
