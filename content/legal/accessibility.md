**The Art of Connect, LLC** ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us") wants every adult to be able to use our website and services comfortably, including people who use assistive technology or have vision, hearing, motor or cognitive disabilities.

## 1. Our goal

We are working toward conformance with the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA**, published by the World Wide Web Consortium.

We **do not currently claim** that the website or services conform to WCAG 2.2 AA. We have not yet completed a formal accessibility audit. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether and when to commission an independent third-party accessibility audit.]**

## 2. What we have built so far

Accessibility features already built into the website include:

- **Keyboard navigation.** Menus, forms, buttons and the membership builder can be used with a keyboard.
- **Visible focus states.** The element you are on is clearly outlined when you navigate by keyboard.
- **Reduced motion.** If your device is set to reduce motion, animations and moving effects are switched off.
- **Text alternatives.** Meaningful images have text descriptions, and decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
- **Responsive layouts.** Pages adapt to phones, tablets and computers and support browser zoom.
- **Readable design.** We choose text sizes, spacing and color contrast with legibility in mind, and we label form fields and error messages clearly.
- **Semantic structure.** Pages use headings, landmarks and labels so screen readers can navigate them.

## 3. Known limitations

Because we have not completed an audit, some areas may not yet meet our goal. These may include:

- color contrast on some decorative or brand-styled elements;
- complex interactive elements, such as the membership builder, with some screen reader and browser combinations;
- third-party features such as payment pages (Stripe), identity verification and bot-protection checks (Cloudflare Turnstile), whose accessibility is managed by those providers;
- older documents or images that may lack full text alternatives.

We will update this statement as we find and fix issues.

## 4. Security checks

To protect members, some forms use an automated bot check. It is designed to run without a puzzle for most people. If a check stops you from signing in, joining or contacting us, email us and we will help you directly.

## 5. Requesting help or an alternate format

If you have difficulty using any part of our website or services, or need a document such as a policy or agreement in another format (for example large print or plain text), contact us:

- **Email:** info@theartofconnect.com
- Include the page or feature, what you were trying to do, and the assistive technology you use, if any.

## 6. Ongoing work

We plan to:

- test new features for keyboard and screen reader use before release;
- review color contrast and text sizing across the site;
- include accessibility in our design and development checklists;
