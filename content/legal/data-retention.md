This policy explains how **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("we," "us") decides how long to keep information and how you can close your account and request deletion. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## 1. Principles
1. We keep personal information only as long as it is needed to provide the service, meet legal and financial obligations, keep the community safe, and resolve disputes.
2. Sensitive information — private journals, assessments, romantic preferences, private communications and identity verification results — receives the shortest practical retention.
3. Where we must keep a record (for example, for tax or payment purposes), we keep only what the obligation requires.
4. We do not keep ID document images at all; identity checks are handled by Stripe Identity.

## 3. Closing your account
You can ask to close your account at any time by writing to **info@theartofconnect.com** from the email address on your account. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: in-account self-service deletion]**

Before closing an account with an active paid membership, please cancel your membership (see the [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy)). Closing an account does not by itself issue a refund.

When your account is closed:
1. You are signed out of all devices.
2. Your profile is removed from matching, directories and introductions.
3. Your private journals, assessments and preferences are deleted, subject to any grace period above.
4. Records we must keep (for example, payment records and agreement acceptances) are retained only for the required period and then deleted or de-identified.

## 4. Couples
Each partner has a separate account. Closing your account does not close your partner's account. Content you added to a shared relationship space may remain visible to your partner. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED / ATTORNEY REVIEW: what happens to shared-space content when one partner leaves]**

## 5. Professionals
Professionals who close their accounts are removed from the directory. Records about appointments and client relationships may be subject to professional record-keeping obligations that belong to the professional, not to The Art of Connect. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW]**

## 6. Legal holds
We may keep information longer when required by law, a court order, an ongoing investigation, or to establish or defend legal claims.

## 7. De-identified information
We may keep aggregated or de-identified information that can no longer reasonably identify you, for example total membership counts.

## 8. Requests and timelines
We will confirm receipt of deletion requests and respond within the period required by applicable law. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: state response timelines and verification standards]**

## 9. Deleting specific content without closing your account
You do not have to close your account to remove information. You can:
- delete individual journal entries and assessment results from your private space;
- remove profile details you no longer want shown;
- withdraw optional consents, such as marketing email or partner sharing; and
- ask us to remove you from the notify-me list.

Deleted content is removed from your account immediately and from our active systems as described above, subject to backups.

## 10. Information others hold
Some information about you may be held by others: a match may remember a conversation, a professional may keep their own client records under their professional obligations, and Stripe keeps payment and verification records under its own terms. Our deletion process covers information held by The Art of Connect and asks our service providers to delete information they hold on our behalf.

## 11. Changes
We will update this policy as retention decisions are approved and will notify members of material changes.
