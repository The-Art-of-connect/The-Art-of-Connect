The Art of Connect is a community for adults aged **21 and older**; The Personal Society singles experience is for adults aged **30 and older**. This policy explains how **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("we," "us") confirms age eligibility and identity, what information is involved, and what verification can and cannot tell you.

## 1. Why we verify
Verification helps keep our community authentic and safe. It reduces fake profiles, impersonation and romance scams, and it supports our age requirement.

## 2. Age eligibility at registration
When you register, you provide your date of birth. Our system will not create a consumer membership account for anyone younger than the minimum age, which is currently 21 (30 for The Personal Society singles experience). The minimum ages are set by the owner and can be raised.

Your date of birth is stored in a private record that only you can read. Our staff, including the owner, see only whether your account meets the age requirement. Your date of birth is never shown on your profile and is never included in emails.

## 3. Identity verification
Some features or memberships may require identity verification, for example before introductions in In Love or before a professional is listed. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: which memberships or features require identity verification, and whether it is required at registration]**

Identity verification is performed by **Stripe Identity**, a third-party provider. During verification you may be asked to:
- photograph a government-issued photo ID; and
- take a live selfie so the provider can compare it with your ID photo.

**The Art of Connect does not receive or store images of your ID or your selfie.** Stripe processes them under its own privacy terms. We receive only:
- the verification outcome (for example, verified, requires more information, or not verified);
- the date of the check; and
- a reference to the verification session so we can resolve problems.

Where the provider confirms a date of birth, we use it only to confirm age eligibility.

## 4. Consent
Before verification begins, we will tell you what the provider will collect and ask you to agree. Verification consent is separate from other agreements and from marketing consent. If you do not consent, you can still use features that do not require verification.

**[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether verification is included in membership or charged separately]** Identity verification charges are not active and will not be activated without the owner's authorization.

## 6. Professionals
Counselors, coaches and mentors go through a separate credential review described in the [Professional Provider Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement), which may include license verification with the relevant licensing board, disciplinary checks, insurance confirmation and references.

## 7. Limits of verification
Verification confirms that a person matched an identity document at a point in time. **It does not:**
- guarantee that a person is honest, safe, single, financially secure, or who they claim to be in every respect;
- reveal criminal history (we do not currently conduct criminal background checks **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED / ATTORNEY REVIEW]**);
- guarantee compatibility or the outcome of any relationship or business connection.

Always follow our [Member Safety and Reporting Policy](/legal/safety-policy), especially before meeting someone in person or sending money.

## 8. Failed or unresolved verification
If verification fails or cannot be completed, features that require it remain unavailable. You may try again. If we believe an account is using false identity information, we may suspend or close it under our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards). You may appeal by writing to info@theartofconnect.com.

## 9. Misrepresentation
Lying about your age or identity, using another person's documents, or helping someone bypass verification violates our Terms and will result in account removal.

## 10. Couples
Each partner in a Couples Membership has a separate account and, where verification is required, completes verification individually. One partner's verification result is never shown to the other unless that partner chooses to share it.

## 11. Verification badges
**[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether verified members display a badge to others]** If badges are used, they will show only that verification was completed — never the document type, document details or date of birth — and members will be reminded of the limits described in section 7.

If you cannot complete automated verification because of a disability, lack of a qualifying ID, or a technical problem, contact info@theartofconnect.com. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED / ATTORNEY REVIEW: alternative verification method and accessibility accommodations]**

## 13. Retention

## 14. Reporting suspected false identity
If you believe a member is using a false identity, misrepresenting their age, or impersonating someone, use the Report option on their profile or email info@theartofconnect.com. Do not confront the person or send them money. Our safety team reviews every report and may require the member to verify again, restrict their account during review, or remove them.

## 15. Changes
We will update this policy before changing verification providers or methods, and will ask for renewed consent where required.
