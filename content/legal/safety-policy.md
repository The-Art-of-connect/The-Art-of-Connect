Your safety and dignity matter to us. This policy explains the protections The Art of Connect, LLC ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us") provides across The Professional Society (In Business), The Art of Becoming (In Life) and The Personal Society (In Love), what we ask of you, and what happens when you report a concern. It should be read with our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards) and [Identity and Age Verification Policy](/legal/identity-verification).

## 1. An honest word about safety
We work hard to create a trusted environment for adults aged 21 and older, but no online service can remove every risk. **The Art of Connect cannot guarantee the identity, intentions, honesty or conduct of any member, couple or professional provider**, whether online or in person. Please use the same judgment you would use when meeting anyone new, and use the tools described below.

## 2. The protections we provide
- **Age eligibility.** Consumer membership is limited to adults aged 21 and older, and The Personal Society singles experience to adults aged 30 and older. You confirm your date of birth at registration, and your date of birth is stored privately and never shown on your profile.
- **Identity and age verification.** We may require or offer verification through our third-party provider, Stripe Identity. See section 5 for what verification does and does not mean.
- **Private by design.** Your journals, reflections and assessment answers are private to you. Contact details are not displayed on profiles.
- **Blocking and reporting** tools in every experience.
- **Automated and manual review** of signals such as repeated reports, suspicious sign-up patterns, payment fraud and identity mismatches, which may lead to restrictions while we review an account.
- **Professional credential review** for licensed providers before they are listed (see the [Professional Provider Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement)).
- **Account security** including strong passwords, secure password recovery and two-step verification for our staff.

## 3. Warning signs of romance scams and financial fraud
Be cautious if someone:
- asks for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, wire transfers, loans or help paying for travel, medical bills, customs fees or legal costs;
- introduces an investment, trading platform, cryptocurrency or "guaranteed" business opportunity;
- professes strong feelings very quickly, or pressures you to move the conversation off the platform;
- repeatedly avoids video calls or meeting in person, or gives inconsistent details about their life;
- claims to be working overseas, in the military, on an oil rig or in another situation that prevents meeting;
- asks for personal financial details, account numbers, passwords or verification codes.

**Never send money or share financial information with someone you have met through The Art of Connect.** Our team will never ask for your password or a verification code. If you suspect a scam, stop contact and report the person immediately. You may also report fraud to the FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov and the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center at ic3.gov.

## 4. Meeting in person safely
When you decide to meet someone from The Personal Society or The Professional Society:
1. **Video call first** to confirm the person matches their profile.
2. **Meet in a public place** with other people around, such as a café, restaurant or hotel lobby. Avoid private homes for early meetings.
3. **Tell a friend or family member** where you are going, who you are meeting and when you expect to be home. Consider sharing your location with them.
4. **Arrange your own transportation** to and from the meeting, and do not accept a ride from someone you have just met.
5. **Keep personal details private** — your home address, workplace details and financial information — until you have built genuine trust.
6. **Stay in control** of your drinks and belongings, and limit alcohol.
7. **Trust your instincts.** You may leave at any time, for any reason.
8. **Report** any behavior that concerns you, even if nothing happened.

## 5. Identity and age verification — what it means and its limits
Verification through Stripe Identity compares an identity document with a live image of the person at a single point in time. A "verified" badge means only that **a document appeared to match the person completing the check when the check was performed.** It is **not** a criminal background check, does not confirm someone's character, marital status, finances or intentions, and does not guarantee that the account has not since been shared or compromised. We do not store copies of government identification documents ourselves; see our [Identity and Age Verification Policy](/legal/identity-verification).

## 6. Compatibility assessments are guidance, not guarantees
Assessments and compatibility insights are designed to support reflection and conversation. They are not psychological diagnoses or predictions, and they cannot guarantee romantic compatibility, a successful relationship or a successful business relationship. See our [Dating and Matchmaking Disclaimer](/legal/matchmaking-disclaimer).

## 7. How to block
You may block any member or provider from the member area at any time. Blocking:
- takes effect **immediately**;
- is **private** — the other person is not notified;
- prevents them from viewing your profile, appearing in your introductions or contacting you through the platform.

You can review and remove blocks in your safety settings.

## 8. How to report
Report a profile, message, appointment, review or behavior using the report option in the member area, or email **info@theartofconnect.com**. Helpful details include what happened, when, and any screenshots — but you do not need evidence or certainty to report a concern.
- Reports are **confidential.** We do not tell the reported person who reported them unless required by law.
- You may report conduct that happened off the platform, including at an in-person meeting, if it involves another member.
- Professional misconduct by a provider can be reported the same way; we may also refer it to the relevant licensing board.

## 9. How we respond
1. **Triage.** We prioritize reports involving threats, violence, minors, sexual misconduct, fraud and self-harm.
2. **Protective steps.** Where there is a credible risk, we may immediately restrict or suspend the reported account while we investigate.
3. **Review.** Trained team members review the report, relevant account activity and any history of prior reports. Staff access is limited to what is needed for the investigation and is recorded in our audit logs. Private journals and assessment answers are not accessed for investigations.
4. **Decision.** Outcomes may include no action, a warning, removal of content, feature limits, suspension or permanent closure, as described in our Community Standards.
5. **Follow-up.** We will let you know we have received your report. For privacy reasons we may not be able to share the specific action taken against another account.

## 12. Emergencies and crisis support
The Art of Connect is **not an emergency service** and does not monitor conversations in real time. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call **911**. For emotional crisis or thoughts of suicide, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). For domestic violence support, contact the National Domestic Violence Hotline at 1-800-799-7233.
