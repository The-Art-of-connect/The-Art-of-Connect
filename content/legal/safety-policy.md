**If you are in immediate danger, call 911.** For emotional crisis support in the U.S., call or text **988**.

## How we protect members
- **Age and identity verification:** members are adults 40+. We may require identity and age verification through Stripe Identity before members take part in In Love matchmaking. We do not store identity documents.
- **Professional verification:** licensed professionals' credentials are verified with the issuing board before approval.
- **Reporting and blocking:** you can report a concern or block a member at any time. Blocking hides you from each other and closes any connection.
- **Review and enforcement:** accounts with repeated reports are automatically flagged for review. We may restrict, suspend or remove accounts.
- **Fraud prevention:** we monitor for scams and limit automated activity.

## What we do not do
Unless stated otherwise, **we do not conduct criminal background checks** on members. Verification confirms identity and age only. [OWNER/COUNSEL: update if background screening is added.]

## Staying safe
- Never send money or financial details to someone you met online.
- Meet in public, tell a friend your plans, and arrange your own transportation.
- Keep conversations on the platform until you're comfortable.
- Trust your instincts — and report anything that concerns you.
