This policy explains how recurring memberships and professional provider subscriptions with **The Art of Connect, LLC** renew, what we disclose before you subscribe, and how to stop renewal. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service) and [Membership Agreement](/legal/membership-agreement). **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: state automatic-renewal law requirements, e.g. California, New York]**

## 1. Which plans renew automatically
- All paid member memberships (individual, Couples, bundles, Complete Society, Executive Private and Elite Concierge).
- All professional provider subscriptions (Standard, Professional and Premium Provider).

Each plan is billed either **monthly** or **annually**, as you select at checkout.

## 2. What we show you before you subscribe
Before you are charged, checkout clearly displays, close to the payment button:

- the membership name and what it includes;
- the recurring price and billing frequency (per month or per year), plus any taxes;
- any one-time onboarding fee, if one applies;
- any promotional price, how long it lasts, and the regular price that follows;
- that the subscription **continues until you cancel**;
- the date of your first renewal charge;
- how to cancel online.

## 3. Your affirmative consent
We will not start a recurring subscription unless you **actively agree** to the automatic renewal terms, for example by ticking a box that is **not pre-selected** and confirming your purchase. We keep a record of your consent, including the version of this policy, the date and time, and the plan you selected.

## 4. Confirmation after purchase
After you subscribe we send an email confirmation that includes the automatic renewal terms, the price and billing frequency, the cancellation method, and information on how to contact us. You can also view these details at any time under **My membership**.

## 5. When and how you are charged
- **Monthly plans** renew on the same day each month as your start date (or the closest valid date).
- **Annual plans** renew once a year on your start anniversary.
- Charges are made to the payment method on file through our payment processor, Stripe. You can update your payment method under **My membership**.

## 6. Reminders before renewal
- **Annual plans:** we send a reminder email before each annual renewal with the renewal date, price and how to cancel. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: reminder timing — 15 to 45 days before annual renewal suggested; ATTORNEY REVIEW: state-specific notice windows]**
- **Free trials or promotional periods**, if offered: we tell you before the regular price begins. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether free trials will be offered]**

## 7. Price changes
If the price of your plan changes, we will email you before the new price applies to your next renewal and give you the chance to cancel first. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: advance notice period for price changes — at least 30 days recommended for attorney review]**

## 8. How to stop automatic renewal
- Go to **My membership** and select **Cancel membership**. You can do this online at any time, in a few steps, without contacting us.
- Cancellation takes effect at the **end of your current billing period**; you keep access until then and are not charged again.
- We send an email confirming your cancellation.
- You can also email info@theartofconnect.com and we will process your request.

**[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: any cancellation fee — none assumed]**

## 9. Failed renewals
If a renewal payment fails, the processor retries the charge and we email you. Paid features may be suspended if payment cannot be collected after the retry period. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: length of grace period before suspension]**

## 10. Refunds
Renewal charges are handled under our [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).

## 11. Changing plans or billing frequency
- You can switch between monthly and annual billing, add or remove experiences, or move between individual, bundled and exclusive memberships from **My membership**.
- When you change plans, the new plan continues to renew automatically on its own billing cycle. Checkout shows the new recurring price and renewal date before you confirm.
- The difference in price for the current period is **prorated** by our payment processor and appears on your next invoice.

## 12. Bundles, couples and exclusive memberships
- A bundle (for example Dual Society or Complete Society) is **one subscription** with one renewal date, not separate subscriptions for each experience.
- In a **Couples Membership**, each partner keeps a separate individual account. The renewal terms, price and cancellation method are shown to the partner who purchases the membership. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: which partner is billed and whether both partners must consent to renewal]**
- Executive Private and Elite Concierge renew on the same terms as other memberships unless different terms are shown at checkout.

## 13. Professional provider subscriptions
Standard, Professional and Premium Provider subscriptions renew automatically on the same basis. Providers receive the same disclosures, confirmations, reminders and online cancellation option described in this policy. A provider's subscription is separate from any fees their clients pay for sessions.

## 14. Records we keep
For each subscription we keep a record of the plan, price, billing frequency, the disclosures shown, your consent (version, date and time), confirmation and reminder emails sent, and any cancellation. These records help resolve questions about charges and are kept under our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).

## 15. Your rights
**Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under applicable law**, including any right to cancel or receive a refund provided by state automatic-renewal, dating-service or consumer-protection laws.
