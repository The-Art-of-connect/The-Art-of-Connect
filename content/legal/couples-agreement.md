This Couples Membership Agreement ("Agreement") applies when two people use a couples membership offered by The Art of Connect, LLC ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us"), including the Couples Membership in **The Personal Society** (In Love) and any couples option added to a bundled membership. It supplements our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service), [Membership Agreement](/legal/membership-agreement), [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy) and [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards). Each partner must accept this Agreement individually.

## 1. Who may join
- Both partners must be adults aged 40 or older (or any higher minimum age we set) and must meet all other eligibility requirements.
- Both partners must be in a committed relationship with each other and must each **freely and independently choose** to participate. No one may be enrolled in a couples membership without their own consent.
- We may ask either partner to complete identity and age verification.

## 2. Two separate individual accounts
- **Each partner has their own account, email address and password.** Accounts may not be shared, and one partner may not log in as the other.
- Each partner accepts our terms and privacy choices for themselves, and each controls their own notification, privacy and marketing preferences.
- Each partner may report or block any member, and may contact us privately, without the other partner's knowledge.

## 3. Your private information stays yours
- **Nothing is shared automatically.** Your private journal entries, personal reflections, individual assessment answers and messages with professionals remain private to you unless you choose to share them.
- The couples experience provides a **shared space** for content that both partners deliberately add. You decide, item by item, what to place there.
- You may stop sharing at any time. Content you have already shared may remain visible to your partner where it has been copied into the shared space; you may remove items you contributed. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether removing shared content removes it from the partner's view immediately, and whether shared-space history is retained.]**
- Our staff do not disclose one partner's private information to the other partner, and couples features are never used to monitor a partner.
- Your private information is never sold, used for advertising or used for artificial intelligence training, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## 4. Independent consent
- Consent to connect as a couple must be given separately by each partner, through their own account.
- Either partner may withdraw consent and disconnect the couple link at any time, without the other partner's agreement.
- If you feel pressured to participate or to share information, please contact us at info@theartofconnect.com. You may also disconnect privately.

## 5. Billing and responsibility for payment
- A couples membership is purchased by one partner (the **"purchasing account"**). The purchasing account holder is responsible for all charges, renewals, upgrades and cancellations, under our [Membership Agreement](/legal/membership-agreement), [Subscription and Automatic Renewal Policy](/legal/subscription-auto-renewal) and [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).
- The other partner's access to couples features depends on the purchasing account's membership remaining active.
- Billing information belongs to the purchasing account and is not visible to the other partner.
- **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether partners may split payment or transfer the purchasing role.]**

## 6. Couples tools are not counseling
Guided conversations, exercises and relationship content are educational and personal-development resources, not couples therapy, marriage counseling or clinical treatment. If you choose to work with a licensed professional through the platform, that relationship is governed by our [Counseling, Coaching and Wellness Disclaimer](/legal/counseling-disclaimer) and the provider's own terms. **The Art of Connect cannot guarantee the success of any relationship.**

## 7. Safety within the relationship
If you are experiencing abuse, coercion or threats from your partner:
- you may disconnect the couple link and change your password at any time;
- you may contact us privately at info@theartofconnect.com, and we will not inform your partner that you contacted us;
- in an emergency, call **911**; the National Domestic Violence Hotline is available at 1-800-799-7233; for emotional crisis, call or text **988**.

The Art of Connect is not an emergency service.

## 8. If you separate or one partner leaves
- Either partner may leave the couples membership at any time by disconnecting the couple link.
- After disconnection, each partner keeps their own individual account and private content. Neither partner receives access to the other's private content.
- **Shared-space content:** **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: what happens to shared-space content after separation — e.g. each partner keeps a copy of what they contributed, or the shared space is closed for both.]**
- **Membership and billing:** the purchasing account remains responsible for the membership until it is changed or cancelled. The purchasing partner may downgrade to an individual membership or cancel. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether the non-purchasing partner is offered a transition to their own membership, and on what terms.]**
- A former partner may not use The Personal Society singles features while still holding an active couples link. After separation, a member may choose a singles membership like any other member.

## 9. Ending the couples membership
Cancellation of the couples membership by the purchasing account ends couples features for both partners at the end of the current billing period, subject to our [Cancellation and Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy). Each partner's individual account, private content and data rights continue under our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention). Either partner may delete their own account; doing so does not delete the other partner's account.

## 10. Conduct
Both partners must follow our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards). If one partner's account is suspended or closed for a violation, we will take reasonable steps to preserve the other partner's individual account and privacy.
