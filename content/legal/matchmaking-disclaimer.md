This disclaimer applies to **The Personal Society** (In Love), including singles memberships, couples memberships, compatibility assessments, introductions, concierge or personalized introductions, events and any related features offered by The Art of Connect, LLC ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us"). ## 1. Our purpose
The Personal Society is designed for adults aged 40 and older who want to approach relationships with intention. We offer tools that encourage self-reflection, thoughtful introductions and respectful connection. We are a membership community, not a guarantee of any relationship.

## 2. No guarantee of compatibility or outcome
**The Art of Connect cannot guarantee romantic compatibility, a successful relationship, marriage, a particular number of introductions, a response from any member, or any other outcome.** Attraction and lasting relationships depend on the choices and circumstances of the people involved, which are beyond our control. Your membership fee pays for access to the platform and its features during your membership — not for a result.

## 3. Compatibility assessments and insights
- Assessments, readiness reflections and compatibility insights are tools for reflection and conversation. They are **not** psychological evaluations, clinical diagnoses or scientific predictions of relationship success.
- Results depend on the honesty and completeness of the answers given by each person, which we cannot verify.
- Your individual assessment answers are private. We may use them to suggest introductions as described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy), but we do not show your answers to other members, sell them, or use them for advertising or artificial intelligence training.

## 4. Introductions and personalized matchmaking
- Introductions are suggestions. You are always free to decline, and others are free to decline you.
- Where introductions are made by a person (for example, a concierge), they reflect that person's judgment based on the information available. They are not a professional recommendation or an endorsement of the other member.
- We do not conduct in-depth investigations into members' backgrounds, finances, health or relationship histories.

## 5. We do not screen for everything
- **Identity and age verification**, where completed, confirms only that a document appeared to match the person at the time of the check. It is not a background check and does not confirm someone's marital status, intentions, character or conduct. See our [Identity and Age Verification Policy](/legal/identity-verification).
- **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether background checks are offered.]** Unless and until we state otherwise in writing, **The Art of Connect does not conduct criminal background checks or sex-offender registry screening of members.**
- We rely on members to provide truthful information and on our community to report concerns.

## 6. Responsibility for your own interactions
**The Art of Connect cannot guarantee another person's conduct.** You are responsible for your own decisions about whom you communicate with and meet, what you share and how you protect yourself. Please follow the safe meeting guidance in our [Member Safety and Reporting Policy](/legal/safety-policy), including:
- video calling before meeting;
- meeting in a public place;
- telling a friend where you will be;
- using your own transportation;
- never sending money or sharing financial information.

## 7. Romance scams
Anyone who asks you for money, gift cards, cryptocurrency, loans or investments — however genuine their story appears — should be treated as a likely scam. Stop contact and report them. We will never ask you to send money to another member.

## 8. Relationship status and honesty
Singles memberships are intended for people who are unattached and open to a relationship. Members must be truthful about their relationship status. Couples memberships are for people already in a relationship and are governed by our [Couples Membership Agreement](/legal/couples-agreement). Misrepresenting your status is a violation of our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards).

## 9. Not counseling or therapy
Relationship content, guided conversations and couples tools are educational and personal-development resources. They are not marriage counseling, therapy or clinical treatment. Licensed professional services, if offered through the platform, are provided by independent providers under separate terms — see our [Counseling, Coaching and Wellness Disclaimer](/legal/counseling-disclaimer).
