In Love offers compatibility assessments and introductions designed to help members connect with intention. Please understand:

- **No guarantees.** We do not guarantee any number of introductions, matches, dates, or relationship outcomes.
- **Compatibility is guidance, not prediction.** Assessments reflect what you share and are tools for reflection, not psychological evaluations.
- **Members are responsible for their own interactions.** We cannot control members' conduct and **do not conduct criminal background checks** unless explicitly stated. Identity verification confirms identity and age only.
- **Singles and couples are separate.** Members in the couples path never appear in matchmaking.
- **Use good judgment.** Follow our [Safety Policy](/legal/safety-policy) and report concerns promptly.
