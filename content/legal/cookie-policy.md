This Cookie and Tracking Policy explains how **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("we," "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on theartofconnect.com and within our member, professional and owner portals. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy).

## 1. What cookies are
Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include local storage and scripts that help a page work. "First-party" cookies are set by our own website; "third-party" cookies are set by another company.

## 2. Our approach
We keep tracking to a minimum. Today we use:
- **Essential cookies** that make sign-in and security work; and
- **First-party attribution cookies** that remember which campaign or member referral brought you to us.

We do **not** currently use third-party advertising cookies, advertising pixels, cross-site tracking, or social-media tracking scripts. We do not sell information collected through cookies.

## 3. Cookies we use

### 3.1 Essential (strictly necessary)
| Purpose | Set by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Sign-in session | The Art of Connect (via Supabase Auth) | Keeps you securely signed in and refreshes your session. Without it you cannot use your account. |
| Security checks | The Art of Connect | Supports protections such as two-factor authentication for staff and safe redirects after sign-in. |

These cookies are required for the service to function and cannot be switched off in our settings. You can block them in your browser, but sign-in will not work.

### 3.2 Attribution (first-party)
| Purpose | What it stores | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign source | The marketing campaign details in the link you followed (such as source and campaign name) | **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: confirm attribution cookie lifetime currently set in the website code]** |
| Referral code | A member's referral code from the link you followed, so the referral can be credited | Same as above |

These cookies are set only by our own website, are marked secure and HTTP-only, and are not shared with advertising networks.

### 3.3 Bot protection
When you use sign-in, registration, password recovery, the notify-me list or professional forms, **Cloudflare Turnstile** runs a privacy-focused check to tell people from automated bots. Cloudflare may process technical information about your browser and connection to perform this check. Cloudflare's own privacy terms apply to that processing.

### 3.4 Payments and identity verification
When you pay for a membership you are taken to **Stripe** Checkout, and if identity verification is required you use **Stripe Identity**. Stripe sets its own cookies on its pages for fraud prevention and security. Stripe's privacy terms apply to those pages.

## 7. What we never do with cookies
- We do not use cookies to read or infer the contents of your private journals, assessments, romantic preferences or messages.
- We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles or to show you advertising on other websites.
- We do not allow advertising networks or data brokers to place cookies through our website.
- We do not sell or share information collected through cookies for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 8. Member, professional and Owner Portal areas
Private areas of the website (your member dashboard, the professional portal and the Owner Portal) use only essential cookies. These pages are marked so that browsers and search engines do not store or index them, and they are never cached by our hosting provider. Staff who sign in to the Owner Portal must also complete two-factor authentication; the security state of that session is held in an essential cookie.

## 9. Sharing a device
If you share a computer or tablet with someone else, sign out when you finish so that your session cookie is removed. You can also ask us to sign your account out of every device by writing to info@theartofconnect.com.
