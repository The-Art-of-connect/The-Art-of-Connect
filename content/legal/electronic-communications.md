This agreement explains how **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("we," "us") communicates with you electronically and how you can give, record and withdraw consent online. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service). **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: E-SIGN Act and state UETA disclosure requirements]**

## 1. Consent to electronic records and signatures
By creating an account, you agree that:

- agreements, policies, disclosures, receipts and notices may be provided to you **electronically**;
- your acceptance online (for example, ticking a box and selecting a button) is your **electronic signature** and has the same effect as a handwritten signature;
- electronic records we keep of your acceptance are valid evidence of your agreement.

## 2. What we send electronically
- **Account messages:** account creation, sign-in alerts, password resets and two-step verification.
- **Billing messages:** receipts, renewal reminders, failed-payment notices, price-change notices, refunds and cancellation confirmations.
- **Legal notices:** updates to our agreements and policies, and requests to accept new versions.
- **Safety and service messages:** reports you file, actions on your account, appeals and important service changes.
- **Appointment messages:** reminders for sessions you book with independent professionals. These never include private details such as the topic of your session.

These messages are part of the service and are sent even if you do not consent to marketing.

## 3. How we deliver them
We deliver communications by email to the address on your account, through notices in your member dashboard, and on our website. A notice is treated as received when sent to your email address or posted in your account. Our transactional email is delivered by our email provider, Resend.

## 4. What we never include in email
For your privacy, emails never include the contents of your private journal, your assessment answers, private messages, session notes or other confidential information. Emails link you back to your secure account instead.

## 5. Optional marketing communications
- Marketing messages (news, events, offers) are **optional** and sent only if you separately opt in.
- The marketing choice is **never pre-selected** and is separate from accepting our agreements. Declining marketing does not affect your membership.
- You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any marketing email or in your account settings.

## 6. Other optional permissions
Some features ask for additional, optional permission, for example sharing selected information with your partner in a Couples Membership or allowing a professional to receive a referral. Each permission is requested separately, explained in plain language, and can be withdrawn in your account settings.

## 7. Hardware and software requirements
To receive and keep electronic records you need: a device with internet access; a current version of a major web browser; an email account; and the ability to view, download or print web pages or PDF files. If our requirements change in a way that affects your ability to access records, we will tell you.

## 8. Keeping your email address current
You must keep a working email address on your account. Update it in your account settings. If messages to your address are returned as undeliverable, we may restrict some features until you update it.

## 9. Getting paper copies
You can download or print any current or prior version of our agreements from the Legal & Policy Center. You may also request a copy of a document you accepted by emailing info@theartofconnect.com. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether a fee will be charged for paper copies — none assumed]**

## 10. Withdrawing consent to electronic records
You may withdraw consent to receive records electronically by emailing info@theartofconnect.com. Because the Services are delivered online, withdrawing this consent means we may need to close your account. Withdrawal does not affect the validity of records provided before you withdrew.

## 11. Records of your consent
We keep a record of each agreement and permission you accept or withdraw, including the document version, date and time, and technical information about the acceptance (such as a one-way hashed network identifier). These records are kept even when documents are later updated, subject to our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).

## 12. Protecting you from fraudulent messages
- We will **never** ask for your password, two-step verification code or full card number by email, text or phone.
- Genuine messages from us come from addresses at theartofconnect.com and link only to theartofconnect.com.
- If you receive a suspicious message that appears to come from us or from another member, do not click its links. Forward it to info@theartofconnect.com or report it from your account.
- Security-related notices, such as a new sign-in or a password change, are sent even if you have turned off other notifications.

## 13. Communications between members and professionals
Messages you exchange with other members or with independent professionals inside the Services are electronic communications between you and them. We store them securely to provide the Services and to investigate safety reports, as described in our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy). We do not use private communications for advertising or to train artificial-intelligence models.

## 14. Timing and language
Notices are effective when sent or posted, even if you do not read them right away. Time-sensitive notices, such as a price change or an annual renewal reminder, are sent within the timeframes described in our [Subscription and Automatic Renewal Policy](/legal/subscription-auto-renewal). Communications are provided in English. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: whether any other languages will be supported]**

## 15. Changes to this agreement
If we change how we deliver electronic records, or the hardware and software you need, we will tell you before the change takes effect and, where required, ask you to accept the updated agreement.
