## 1. What The Professional Society is
The Professional Society is a private networking community for accomplished adults aged 21 and older. It helps members discover people with complementary experience, exchange ideas, find mentors and explore possible collaborations. It is a place to meet people — not a source of professional advice, a marketplace for securities or an employment agency.

- Nothing provided through The Professional Society — including introductions, compatibility insights, content, events, member conversations or mentor discussions — is **investment, securities, legal, accounting, tax, financial, insurance or employment advice.**
- Information shared by members and mentors reflects their own views and experience. They are not acting on behalf of The Art of Connect.
- Before making any business, investment, legal, financial or career decision, consult a qualified, licensed professional who can consider your circumstances.

## 3. Not a securities or investment platform
- The Art of Connect is **not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, funding portal, crowdfunding platform or exchange,** and does not facilitate, recommend or participate in the offer or sale of securities or investments.
- **Members may not use The Professional Society to solicit investments,** offer securities, raise capital from members or promote trading, cryptocurrency or "guaranteed return" schemes. Such conduct violates our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards) and may be unlawful.
- If someone pitches you an investment through the platform, decline, stop contact and report it.

- Put important agreements in writing and seek independent professional advice.
