This policy explains how to cancel a membership or professional provider subscription with **The Art of Connect, LLC**, and how refunds, billing errors and payment disputes are handled. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service), [Membership Agreement](/legal/membership-agreement) and [Subscription and Automatic Renewal Policy](/legal/subscription-auto-renewal).

## 1. Cancelling online
- Sign in and open **My membership**, then select **Cancel membership**. The process is online, takes only a few steps and does not require calling or emailing us.
- You can also email info@theartofconnect.com from the address on your account and we will cancel for you.
- You receive an email confirming the cancellation and the date your access ends.

## 2. What happens when you cancel
- Cancellation stops all **future renewals**.
- You keep access to your membership until the **end of the billing period you have already paid for**. After that, paid features end.
- Your account, profile and private journal remain available to you unless you choose to delete your account. See our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).
- You can rejoin at any time at the price shown at checkout then.

## 5. How approved refunds are handled
- Refunds are issued only to the **original payment method**. We cannot refund to a different card, bank account or person.
- If a refund is approved for part of a billing period, the amount and how it was calculated are shown in the confirmation email.
- Taxes collected on a refunded charge are refunded with it, where applicable.
- Promotional discounts are not paid out as cash. If a discounted charge is refunded, the refund equals the amount actually paid.
- Each refund is recorded in our financial records with the date, amount and the team member who approved it.

## 6. Cancelling versus deleting your account
- **Cancelling** your membership stops renewals; your account and personal records remain so you can return.
- **Deleting** your account removes your profile and personal content as described in our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention). Deleting an account also cancels any active membership. Some billing and consent records must be kept for legal and accounting reasons.
- Deleting your account does not by itself entitle you to a refund of the current period.

## 7. Billing errors
If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged the wrong amount, contact us and we will correct the error and refund the incorrect amount.

## 8. Upgrades and downgrades
When you change memberships, the price difference is **prorated** automatically for the rest of the current period and shown on your next invoice. This is not a separate refund.

## 10. Professional provider subscriptions
Providers cancel the same way, from their account. Fees paid by clients for sessions with independent providers are covered by the [Professional Provider Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement) and the provider's own cancellation terms.

## 11. Chargebacks and payment disputes
- Please **contact us first** at info@theartofconnect.com. Most billing concerns can be resolved quickly without a bank dispute.
- If you file a chargeback, we may **suspend your account while the dispute is open** and share the transaction and account records the payment processor needs to review it.
- If a chargeback is resolved in our favour, outstanding amounts remain due before paid access is restored.
- Filing a dispute never removes rights you have under applicable law.
