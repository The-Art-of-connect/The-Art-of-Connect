This policy explains how intellectual property works on the services of **The Art of Connect, LLC** ("The Art of Connect," "we," "us"), and how to report content you believe infringes your copyright. It is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service).

## 1. What The Art of Connect owns

The Art of Connect owns, or is licensed to use, the platform and its content, including:

- the names, logos, monograms, taglines and visual identity of **The Art of Connect**, **The Professional Society**, **The Art of Becoming** and **The Personal Society**, including "In Business. In Life. In Love.";
- the website, apps, software, design, layouts and graphics;
- written, audio and video content we create, including guided reflections, prompts, assessments, educational materials, emails and marketing;
- the structure and wording of our compatibility, readiness and emotional-intelligence assessments.

## 2. What you may do with our content

As a member, you receive a limited, personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable license to use our content for your own personal use while your membership is active. You may not:

- copy, sell, republish, distribute or create derivative works from our content;
- use our names, logos or brand elements in any way that suggests endorsement or affiliation without our written permission;
- scrape, harvest or bulk-download content or member information;
- reverse-engineer or copy the platform, its software or its assessments.

## 3. What you own

You keep ownership of the content you create, such as your profile text, photos, messages and journal entries ("Your Content").

You grant The Art of Connect a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to host, store, display and process Your Content **only as needed to operate, secure and provide the services to you**. For example, we display your profile to the members you choose to be visible to.

This license:

- does **not** allow us to sell Your Content;
- does **not** allow us to use your private journal entries, assessment answers or private communications for advertising or to train artificial-intelligence models;
- ends when you delete Your Content or your account, except for copies we must keep for legal, security or backup reasons as described in our [Data Retention and Account Deletion Policy](/legal/data-retention).

**Private journal entries stay private.** They are visible only to you unless you choose to share them.

You must have the rights to anything you upload. Do not upload photos of other people, or content created by others, without permission.

## 4. Professional providers' content

Counselors, coaches and mentors keep ownership of the materials they create and grant us a limited license to display their profiles and approved materials, as described in the [Professional Provider Agreement](/legal/professional-agreement).

## 5. Feedback

If you send us ideas or suggestions, we may use them to improve the services without any obligation to you. We will not publish your name with your feedback without permission.

## 6. Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe content on the platform infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated copyright agent containing:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the copyrighted work you believe was infringed;
3. identification of the infringing material and where it appears on the platform, with enough detail for us to find it;
4. your name, address, telephone number and email address;
5. a statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent or the law;
6. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

**Designated copyright agent:** The Art of Connect, LLC, **info@theartofconnect.com**. **[OWNER DECISION REQUIRED: designated copyright agent name and mailing address, and registration of the agent with the U.S. Copyright Office's DMCA Designated Agent Directory.]**

We may remove or disable access to the material and will notify the member who posted it.

## 7. Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to our copyright agent including:

1. your physical or electronic signature;
2. identification of the removed material and where it appeared;
3. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed by mistake or misidentification;
4. your name, address and telephone number, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, if outside the United States, any judicial district where The Art of Connect may be found) and will accept service of process from the person who filed the original notice.

If we receive a valid counter-notice, we may restore the material after the waiting period required by law unless the original complainant notifies us that they have filed a court action. **[ATTORNEY REVIEW: DMCA safe-harbor requirements and process timing.]**

## 8. Repeat infringers

We may suspend or close the accounts of members who repeatedly infringe others' intellectual property.

To report misuse of a trademark, impersonation or other intellectual-property concerns, email **info@theartofconnect.com** with details. Impersonation of members is also covered by our [Community Standards](/legal/community-standards).
