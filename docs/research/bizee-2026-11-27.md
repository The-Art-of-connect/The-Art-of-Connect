## 6. Recommendation framing

| Option | Member experience | Effort and requirements | Revenue | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Affiliate (Awin)** | Off-site: members leave for bizee.com, and the account and documents are at Bizee | Days: apply on Awin, add disclosed links | Commission per sale (amount unconfirmed), 30-day cookie | Launching quickly to test demand |
| **B. Bizee Platform API** | Embedded: intake and status on your site; Bizee files, with webhooks for status | Needs a signed agreement, a developer integration, and handling PII and payments. Terms, minimums and security posture not public | Wholesale invoicing, so you set the retail markup | A core, high-volume feature |
| **C. In-house guidance + free state portals** | Your guides and checklists, then a link to the Secretary of State's site; members file themselves | Content work only, with ongoing state-by-state upkeep. No registered agent or EIN service unless you add one (EIN is free at IRS.gov) | None directly (membership value) | Budget-minded members; avoids a vendor dependency |

Suggested path: launch **A + C** now (guides plus an affiliate link for members who want it done for them). At the same time, use the partnership form to ask about **B**, and choose B only if its terms, security attestations and API coverage (orders, status, documents, registered agent, annual reports) meet your needs. Present Bizee as a third-party provider, and do not give legal advice.

## Questions to ask Bizee

1. Can our existing customer account be converted or linked to a Platform partnership, or is a new partner account needed?
2. Which integration paths exist (headless API, embedded or hosted flow, white-label), and which fits a membership site?
3. Are there partner minimums (volume, setup fees, contract term)?
4. What is the wholesale price list per service (formation, registered agent, EIN, operating agreement, annual reports, BOIR), and who collects the state fees?
5. Which API endpoints exist for order creation, status, document retrieval (stamped articles, EIN confirmation letter CP 575), and registered-agent mail?
6. Which webhook events exist, and how are they signed?
7. Sandbox access: what is required, and does it truly match production?
8. Do you have SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001? Will you sign a DPA? Where is data stored and for how long?
9. Who is the customer of record: our member, us, or both? Who handles support and refunds?
10. How do state coverage and entity types (LLC, C-corp, S-corp election, nonprofit with 501(c)(3)) differ between the API and the consumer site?
11. Branding: can it be white-labeled, or is "powered by Bizee" required?
12. Affiliate: exact commission schedule, validation period, payout threshold, disclosure requirements, and whether affiliate and platform can run together.
