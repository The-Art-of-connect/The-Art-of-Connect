# 18 — DISCOVER WHAT'S POSSIBLE

_Founder directive, November 26, 2026, kept word for word. Status: **built** — Discover What's Possible (`/app/discover`) on the Art of Becoming dashboard, What's Happening Inside Connect (owner-approved previews), free-to-trial and trial-to-paid conversions in the Owner Analytics Center._

Peace,

The complimentary Art of Becoming experience should also encourage free members to transition naturally into paid memberships.

Create a personalized membership discovery experience called Discover What’s Possible.

As individuals complete their self-discovery activities, use their authorized preferences to introduce relevant membership opportunities.

Include previews of:

* Business ideas and discussion topics.
* Professional networking opportunities.
* Like-minded friendship communities.
* Social conversations.
* Upcoming events.
* Travel communities.
* Educational webinars.
* Singles and relationship experiences.
* Couples communities.
* Elite qualification opportunities, where appropriate.

Create a daily or weekly section called What’s Happening Inside Connect.

Allow free members to see approved previews of genuine community activities, upcoming discussions and events without accessing private member profiles, private conversations or paid interactive features.

Provide clear invitations to start an eligible 15-day trial or purchase a membership.

Personalize these invitations based on completed self-discovery information using standard preference-based logic without consuming paid AI resources.

Never fabricate community activity, member counts, compatible matches or business opportunities.

The complimentary experience should provide genuine personal development value while helping individuals understand the additional benefits of becoming paying members.

We want people to become curious about who they could meet, what they could learn, what business ideas they could discover and what meaningful experiences they could share.

Integrate this into our existing Art of Becoming dashboard and Connection Intelligence Engine rather than creating a separate system.

Track free-to-trial and trial-to-paid membership conversions within the Owner Analytics Center.

Maintain all existing privacy protections and commercial launch restrictions.
