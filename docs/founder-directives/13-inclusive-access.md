# 13 — COMMUNITY ACCESS PHILOSOPHY: INCLUSIVE, NOT SEGREGATED

_Founder directive, kept word for word. Status: **applied in phase 1** — ordinary circles are open to every eligible member; only Elite circles require verification._

Please clarify our community access philosophy throughout the Social Ecosystem Master Blueprint.

Every member should be allowed to explore, join and interact within the ordinary Connection Circles of their choice.

The Connection Intelligence Engine should recommend communities based on personality, interests, lifestyle, ambitions and goals, but these recommendations must never become mandatory assignments or restrictions.

Do not use income, financial status, professional accomplishments or personality classifications to prevent members from participating in ordinary communities.

Allow members to participate in discussions, share knowledge, attend eligible events, meet new people and explore different lifestyles and interests.

Preserve Elite Concierge as a separate verified membership distinction with exclusive benefits, private experiences and optional verified Elite-only introductions.

Maintain applicable age, consent, safety and privacy requirements.

We are building an inclusive social ecosystem with personalized recommendations, not a system of social segregation.

The system recommends. The individual chooses.
