# 17 — THE ART OF BECOMING DAILY SANCTUARY

_Founder directive, November 26, 2026, kept word for word. Status: **built** — My Daily Sanctuary (`/app/sanctuary`), Curriculum Studio → My Daily Sanctuary. The starter library was prepared by Peace as drafts awaiting founder approval; nothing reaches members until published._

Peace,

I want our complimentary Art of Becoming experience to provide ongoing daily, weekly and monthly value.

Free members should have meaningful reasons to return without purchasing a membership or using personalized Journey AI.

Create a comprehensive private personal development experience called My Daily Sanctuary within The Art of Becoming.

Daily Experiences

Include:

* Morning manifestations.
* Daily affirmations.
* Morning intentions.
* Emotional check-ins.
* Daily reflection questions.
* Short educational life lessons.
* Real-life self-discovery scenarios.
* Gratitude journaling.
* Daily personal growth challenges.
* Personal accomplishments.
* Evening reflections.

Educational Content

Develop an owner-managed educational library covering identity, self-love, emotional intelligence, communication, boundaries, relationships, work ethic, financial mindset, personal values, confidence and intentional living.

Provide short lessons containing educational explanations, practical examples, reflection questions and activities.

Use approved prewritten content and responses for complimentary members. Do not activate paid AI services for free accounts.

Progressive Self-Discovery

Use educational scenarios to help members understand their behaviors, communication styles, preferences, values and interpersonal interactions.

With appropriate consent, incorporate relevant answers into their private Personal Discovery Portfolio and Connection Intelligence Engine.

Do not repeat questions that members have already answered unless they voluntarily choose to revisit them.

Do not treat these educational activities as clinical assessments or definitive personality judgments.

Weekly and Monthly Experiences

Include:

* Weekly personal reflection.
* Personal growth challenges.
* Emotional check-in summaries.
* Goal reviews.
* Educational mini-courses.
* Monthly personal development summaries.
* Letters to my future self.
* Accomplishment tracking.
* Discovery Portfolio reviews.

Use deterministic summaries and prewritten educational guidance for free accounts.

My Vision Board

Create a private digital vision board where members can upload photographs, establish goals, write intentions and record accomplishments.

Include categories for career, business, financial freedom, relationships, travel, personal growth, wellness and lifestyle.

Maintain private storage, upload limits and appropriate security protections.

Optional Reminders

Allow members to voluntarily receive daily, weekly or monthly reminders through our existing notification infrastructure.

Allow members to choose the time, frequency and type of reminder.

Do not use guilt, artificial urgency, compulsory streaks or manipulative engagement techniques.

Natural Membership Discovery

Once sufficient authorized self-discovery information exists, show free members examples of relevant community categories and experiences they could explore through an eligible paid membership.

Do not expose private member profiles, unlock paid social functionality or imply that compatible individuals have already been identified when insufficient information exists.

Encourage membership without pressuring members to upgrade.

Preserve our complimentary Art of Becoming experience indefinitely.

Owner Controls

Integrate content creation, scheduling, editing, approval and publication into our existing Curriculum Studio.

Allow me to prepare daily lessons, reflections, affirmations, scenarios and challenges in advance.

Reuse existing infrastructure wherever possible.

Add these requirements to the Social Ecosystem Master Blueprint, Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

Maintain current commercial launch restrictions and obtain my approval before adding paid infrastructure.

Our free experience should help individuals continuously discover, understand and become themselves.

Our paid experience should help them build meaningful connections with others.
