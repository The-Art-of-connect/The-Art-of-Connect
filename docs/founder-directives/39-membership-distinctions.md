Founder Directive — Executive & Elite Membership Distinctions

Executive and Elite members must have distinct, recognizable membership emblems throughout The Art of Connect.

Executive: A sophisticated champagne-gold and charcoal membership emblem.

Elite: A prestigious signature orange-gold and black membership emblem reflecting our highest invitation-only membership.

Display the appropriate membership distinction consistently across authorized member profiles, discovery results, professional networking, CONNECT 360, community circles, messages and collaboration experiences.

Maintain a separate orange identity verification badge. Membership status must not be confused with identity verification, financial verification, professional credential verification or other verified information.

Membership emblems must be controlled by actual server-side membership entitlements. Members must not be able to assign, purchase separately or manually modify their badges.

Automatically update membership distinctions when membership status changes.

Respect member privacy and existing profile visibility permissions.

Elite members retain full access to all applicable services in Life, Love and Business.

Use a consistent, elegant and understated design across mobile and desktop.

Audit existing badge functionality before developing replacements. Preserve all approved security requirements and production restrictions.

Include membership distinction and badge integrity testing in the second independent customer experience audit.
