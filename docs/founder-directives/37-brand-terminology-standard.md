# 37 — PLATFORM-WIDE NAMING STANDARD AND BRAND TERMINOLOGY REGISTRY

_Founder Directive, November 28, 2026, kept word for word. Status: **recorded; a permanent design and development requirement.** Implemented as the Brand Terminology Registry (`src/lib/brand.ts`, Owner Portal → Brand Terminology Registry), enforced by an automatic check (`tests/brand.test.ts`) that runs on every change._

Establish a mandatory platform-wide naming standard for The Art of Connect.

Every approved feature and experience must have one official name used consistently throughout the entire website.

CONNECT 360 must always be called CONNECT 360.

My Intelligence Office must always be called My Intelligence Office.

Apply the same standard to every other approved feature, division, membership and service.

Create a centralized Brand Terminology Registry that controls naming across:

* Desktop and mobile navigation
* Page titles and headings
* Search results
* Homepage cards
* Membership benefits
* Upgrade pop-ups
* Journey text and voice responses
* Emails and notifications
* Owner Portal
* Help Center
* Reports and downloadable documents

Search may recognize synonyms and everyday language, but it must return the official feature name.

Audit all existing pages and correct inconsistent terminology. Do not rename approved features or introduce alternative branding without founder approval.

This is a permanent design and development requirement.

Preserve all functionality, membership permissions, security controls and existing production restrictions.
