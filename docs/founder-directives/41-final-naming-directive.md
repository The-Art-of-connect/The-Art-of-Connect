2. Membership Terminology

* Executive Private is the official membership name.
* Executive Member identifies an active Executive Private membership holder.
* Elite Concierge is the official membership name.
* Elite Member identifies an active Elite Concierge membership holder.

Membership distinctions and identity verification must remain separate.

3. Platform-Wide Implementation

Apply the official names consistently across:

* Desktop and mobile navigation
* Homepage and membership dashboards
* Page titles and headings
* Search results
* Membership descriptions and pricing pages
* Trial, purchase and upgrade pop-ups
* Journey’s text, voice and intelligent navigation
* Emails and notifications
* Member profiles
* Owner Portal
* Help Center

4. Search and Navigation

Search and Journey may recognize synonyms, former names and everyday language to understand member intentions.

However, all feature destinations and displayed names must use the official approved terminology.

For example, a member searching for “research” should be directed to My Intelligence Office, while a member searching for “business funding” should be directed to the Capital & Funding Intelligence Center.

* Member information and saved progress

* Membership entitlements
* Internal page addresses and working links
