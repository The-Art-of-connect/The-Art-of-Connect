# 22 — BUSINESS & TAX EDUCATION CENTER

_Founder directive, November 26, 2026, kept word for word. Extends the Business Intelligence Center (directive 21). Status: **recorded; not built.** Design notes: Journey cannot browse the web, so "current authoritative sources" will come from an owner-approved, dated knowledge base of primary sources (IRS publications and forms, state revenue departments, SBA), each tagged with tax year and jurisdiction, which Journey cites and never goes beyond. Financial document uploads need encrypted private storage with strict limits. Professional referrals are infrastructure only until the owner approves specific professionals and terms._

Peace,

Expand the Business Intelligence Center to include a comprehensive Business & Tax Education Center.

Journey should be capable of educating business owners about business structures, potential tax-saving strategies, business deductions, payroll, retirement plans, depreciation, financial statements, cash flow, business credit, funding, business expansion and succession planning.

Journey should ask relevant questions about the business owner's circumstances before identifying potentially applicable strategies.

Use current authoritative sources, identify the applicable tax year and jurisdiction, and explain eligibility requirements and limitations.

Provide educational guidance, illustrative calculations, planning considerations and questions members can discuss with qualified professionals.

Do not represent Journey as a licensed accountant, tax professional, attorney or financial advisor.

Develop advanced business planning workspaces for Executive and Elite members, including private document organization, business strategy reports, tax planning checklists and professional consultation preparation.

Use appropriate security protections for uploaded financial documents.

Preserve membership usage allowances and Elite all-inclusive platform privileges.

Provide the infrastructure for future referrals to qualified accounting, tax, legal and business professionals, subject to owner approval.

Integrate these capabilities into the existing Business Intelligence Center rather than creating a separate AI service.
