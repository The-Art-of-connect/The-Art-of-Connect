# 10 — SOCIAL ECOSYSTEM — FIND YOUR CIRCLE & CONNECTION INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

_Founder directive, kept word for word. Status: **designed — recorded in the Development Tracker and QA Center; not built**._
_The build blueprint is in [docs/social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md](../social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md)._

I want The Art of Connect to intelligently help members find like-minded individuals and communities without restricting them from interacting with other people.

Create a comprehensive community discovery experience called Find Your Circle, supported by our Connection Intelligence Engine.

## Core Philosophy

Our objective is to help people find their kind of people while remaining free to explore different personalities, lifestyles, interests and experiences.

Do not segregate members based on personality assessments.

Do not automatically assign members to permanent categories or prevent them from entering other eligible communities.

The system recommends. The member decides.

## Connection Intelligence Engine

With appropriate member authorization, utilize relevant information from:

* The Art of Self.
* Personality preferences.
* Personal interests.
* Social preferences.
* Communication preferences.
* Lifestyle choices.
* Professional ambitions.
* Personal values.
* Current life stage.
* Future goals.
* Travel preferences.
* Relationship intentions.
* Member-selected activities and hobbies.

Use this information to recommend relevant communities, individuals, events, experiences and educational opportunities.

Allow members to view, correct and update their connection preferences.

Do not publicly expose private assessment answers, journals, financial verification information or counseling records.

Do not classify members as superior or inferior based on personality, wealth, emotional development or personal circumstances.

## Connection Alignment Levels

Create recommendation categories including:

* Naturally Aligned.
* Shared Interests.
* Growth Connections.
* Explore Beyond Your Circle.
* Open Community.

These categories represent different opportunities for connection, not rankings of human value.

Explain why a community or connection is being recommended.

Allow members to explore and join multiple eligible communities regardless of their recommended alignment category.

Preserve legitimate membership, age, consent and Elite eligibility requirements.

## Connection Circles

Create communities covering:

* Business and entrepreneurship.
* Executive leadership.
* Investing and retirement planning.
* Travel and adventure.
* Personal development.
* Conscious living.
* Manifestations and affirmations.
* Friendships and social activities.
* Music, arts and culture.
* Dining and entertainment.
* Intentional singles.
* Couples and marriage.
* Life transitions.
* Empty nesters.
* Starting over after divorce.
* Fitness and wellness.
* Community involvement.
* Professional mentorship.
* Elite connections.
* Local, national and international communities.

Allow eligible members to create additional circles based on shared interests.

## Individual Community Experiences

Each circle should have its own:

* Community dashboard.
* Member directory.
* Social feed.
* Discussions and questions.
* Photographs and videos.
* Upcoming events.
* Group activities.
* Group messaging.
* Educational resources.
* Shared recommendations.
* Event invitations.
* Community announcements.
* Optional notifications.
* Moderation and reporting tools.

Allow members to share existing external events as well as create their own events.

Integrate all community events with The Connect Calendar.

## Find People Like Me

Create an intelligent discovery feature that helps members find compatible individuals for:

* Friendships.
* Business networking.
* Professional mentorship.
* Travel companionship.
* Shared hobbies.
* Social experiences.
* Couples friendships.
* Romantic relationships, where eligible.

Allow members to indicate what type of connection they are seeking.

Never automatically interpret a friendship or professional connection as romantic interest.

Require mutual consent before establishing private connections where appropriate.

## Journey Integration

For eligible paid members, allow Journey to help them:

* Understand their connection preferences.
* Discover suitable communities.
* Identify relevant upcoming events.
* Explore new interests.
* Discover compatible social opportunities.
* Receive personalized suggestions.
* Adjust recommendations as their interests and goals change.

Free members should retain standard preference-based recommendations and authorized community participation.

## Healthy Social Media Standards

Build a helpful, respectful and intentional social environment.

Avoid recommendation systems designed to promote outrage, harmful comparisons, compulsive scrolling or conflict.

Allow members to control their feeds, recommendation preferences and notifications.

Provide effective blocking, reporting, moderation and appeals.

Encourage constructive conversations, knowledge sharing, meaningful interactions and real-life experiences.

## Owner Management

Create owner controls for:

* Community categories.
* Member-created groups.
* Recommendation rules.
* Community moderation.
* Events and announcements.
* Community visibility.
* Membership access.
* Notification settings.
* Engagement analytics.
* Safety reports.
* Community growth.

Integrate this initiative into our Social Ecosystem Master Blueprint, Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

Review existing functionality before building duplicate systems.

Maintain all commercial launch restrictions and obtain my approval before introducing additional paid infrastructure.

The Art of Connect should help people discover themselves, find their people, build meaningful connections and experience life together.

In Business. In Life. In Love.
