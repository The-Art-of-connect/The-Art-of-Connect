## How to apply this to every future build

- Treat the owner's conversational messages as the **vision**, not the copy. Write member-facing text that is sophisticated, warm and inviting.
- Invite rather than instruct: "Discover…", "Explore…", "Honor…" — never "You must…", "Before you do X, do Y", or anything that sounds like rules for behaving.
- Never judgmental, never demanding. Participation in self-discovery, development, manifestations, counseling or therapy is always optional.
- Visuals: espresso and champagne gold, subtle flowing graphics, cinematic photography, Cormorant display type.
