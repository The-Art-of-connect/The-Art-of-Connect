THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — Lifelong Couples Experience and Professional Independence

The Couples experience must be designed as a lifelong relationship development experience, not a temporary service that ends when members become engaged, married or established in their relationship.

Lifelong Relationship Development

Support members throughout every applicable relationship stage, including dating, exclusive relationships, engagement, marriage, family development, major life transitions and retirement.

Journey must provide ongoing, evolving relationship education, personalized guidance, meaningful activities and opportunities for continued connection.

Include:

* Personalized date night recommendations and romantic surprises.
* Relationship challenges and meaningful conversation starters.
* Engagement and marriage preparation.
* Five Love Languages activities.
* Emotional intelligence and communication development.
* Intimacy and relationship enrichment education.
* Shared goals and important milestones.
* Anniversary recognition.
* Relationship check-ins and personalized growth reports.
* Appropriate referrals to the Counseling & Wellness Center.

Activities and recommendations must evolve with the relationship rather than repeatedly presenting the same material.

In Business Access

Couples must be able to participate in In Business either together or individually, according to their applicable membership entitlements.

Each partner must retain an independent identity, professional profile, personal development history and private information.

Allow eligible members to pursue individual professional opportunities or establish shared business projects through CONNECT 360.

Shared business access must require appropriate consent and authorization.

Membership Progression

Adding professional or business benefits must never remove the Couples experience.

Executive Private and Elite Concierge must accommodate members who are single, engaged, married or in committed relationships.

Elite Concierge includes all applicable platform services across In Life, In Love and In Business.

Relationship status determines the appropriate relationship experience, not the member’s ability to pursue professional growth.

Journey Continuity

Journey must maintain authorized relationship history, recognize important milestones and adapt recommendations as the relationship evolves.

Allow couples to choose their preferred relationship growth reporting frequency and change it through Settings.

Preserve separate private reflections and jointly authorized relationship information.

Journey must remain impartial, respect both partners’ autonomy and avoid revealing confidential individual conversations.

Support healthy relationship decisions without pressuring members to remain in unhealthy or unsafe relationships.

Permanent Requirements

Audit existing Couples functionality before developing additional features.

Integrate new functionality into the existing approved navigation and experiences rather than creating unnecessary menus.

Include all relationship stages, individual and shared business participation, privacy protections and membership progression in Journey’s comprehensive customer audit.

Do not introduce new membership pricing or activate production services without explicit founder approval.

Founder principle: Finding each other is only the beginning. The Art of Connect supports a lifetime of growing together.
