# 42 — INTELLIGENT MEMBERSHIP RECOMMENDATIONS AND UPGRADE PATHWAYS

_Founder Directive, November 28, 2026, kept word for word. Expands directive 36. Status: **recorded; in progress.** Production activation restrictions unchanged._

Founder Directive — Intelligent Membership Recommendations and Upgrade Pathways

Expand Directive 36 to cover every approved membership plan across The Art of Connect, not only Executive Private and Elite Concierge.

Preserve the approved universal membership pop-up design.

Dynamic Upgrade Pop-ups

Every restricted feature must generate the appropriate membership pop-up based on:

* The member’s current membership.
* The feature they attempted to access.
* Their existing entitlements.
* Eligible membership upgrades.
* Current approved membership pricing.
* Complimentary 15-day trial eligibility.

Recommend the next appropriate membership level that actually unlocks the requested feature. Do not automatically recommend Executive Private or Elite Concierge when a lower-priced membership satisfies the member’s needs.

Present relevant higher-value alternatives when appropriate.

Intelligent Membership Selection

Enhance the membership selection and comparison experience with the following dynamic distinctions:

* Current Membership
* Recommended for You
* Best Value
* Most Comprehensive Access

Recommendations must reflect the member’s stated interests, desired experiences, existing membership and required functionality.

Best Value must be based on genuine membership benefits and pricing, not simply the highest-priced subscription.

Include every approved membership plan using the existing centralized pricing and entitlement records.

Clearly explain what the member gains by upgrading, what they already receive and the applicable price difference. Do not imply that an upgrade is required for benefits already included in their membership.

Journey Integration

Journey should assist eligible members in understanding and selecting the most appropriate membership.

Free members should receive rule-based membership recommendations without requiring complimentary personalized AI conversations.

Journey must explain its recommendations and must never automatically purchase, upgrade or change a membership without the member’s authorization.

Elite Concierge

Elite Concierge remains invitation-only and subject to approved eligibility requirements.

Elite Members receive all applicable platform services across In Life, In Love and In Business. Do not display unnecessary membership upgrade offers. Additional Journey usage remains subject to approved allowances and credit purchasing rules.

Testing and Security

Test every membership level against every restricted feature.

Verify trial eligibility, pricing consistency, membership entitlements, upgrade and downgrade behavior, Sandbox checkout, and return to the originally selected feature.

Preserve the official Brand Terminology Registry and all existing security and privacy protections.

Maintain all production activation restrictions.

The objective is to help members select the membership that delivers the greatest genuine value for their individual goals while creating clear and intelligent revenue opportunities for The Art of Connect.
