THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — Dual Intelligence Infrastructure

Peace,

I have decided that our CONNECT 360 Global Intelligence Division should utilize both Parallel Web Systems and Exa.

I do not want these providers operating as separate member-facing services. They must operate collaboratively within our existing Journey infrastructure.

## 1. Journey — Director of Intelligence

Journey should: understand the member's objective; ask appropriate follow-up questions; develop a structured research assignment; determine which research capabilities are required; delegate investigations to Parallel and Exa; evaluate their findings; identify conflicting or missing information; assign additional research when necessary; prepare the final intelligence report; recommend actionable next steps.

## 2. Parallel — Investigative Intelligence

Utilize supported Parallel capabilities for: comprehensive investigations; deep research; organization research; opportunity requirements; public financial information; regulatory research; risk identification; source extraction; continuous monitoring; structured research findings.

## 3. Exa — Discovery Intelligence

Utilize supported Exa capabilities for: professional discovery; organization discovery; opportunity identification; industry-specific discovery; business resource discovery; criteria-based matching; publicly available professional information; structured discovery results; data enrichment; additional opportunity discovery.

## 4. Cross-Verification Engine

Develop a coordinated research workflow that allows the findings of each provider to inform further investigations by the other.

The system should: compare findings; identify conflicting information; verify authoritative sources; identify outdated information; remove duplicate results; investigate missing requirements; distinguish confirmed information from unverified claims; document research limitations; continue investigating when justified and within approved limits.

Agreement between the two providers alone does not constitute independent verification. Verify important findings against reliable underlying sources.

Do not automatically complete an assignment simply because one provider returns a response.

## 5. My Intelligence Office

Create a dedicated premium workspace titled My Intelligence Office.

Allow Executive and Elite members to: submit research assignments; review investigation progress; provide additional instructions; review preliminary findings; request further investigation; review completed intelligence reports; save relevant opportunities; prepare professional introductions; organize private meetings; track outcomes; maintain research history.

Elite members should receive advanced ongoing intelligence capabilities, including continuous monitoring and proactive opportunity discovery.

## 6. Intelligence Reports

Every completed investigation should provide: original objective; research scope; executive summary; detailed findings; relevant opportunities; professional or organizational requirements; available financial information; potential risks; supporting evidence; direct source links; verification dates; unresolved questions; recommended next steps; introduction assistance where appropriate.

Allow reports to be saved, printed and exported professionally using The Art of Connect branding.

## 7. External Connection Growth

Allow members to invite relevant external organizations and professionals through authorized public business channels. Require member approval before outreach. Create secure invitation links for eligible external participants. Integrate with our existing membership, messaging and private meeting infrastructure. Do not facilitate unsolicited mass outreach or collect private contact information.

## 8. Intelligence Cost Management

Develop centralized controls for both providers. Include: cost per assignment; cost per API provider; cost per member; membership-level usage; daily and monthly expenses; research depth controls; maximum investigation stages; concurrent assignment limits; monitoring frequency; shared research caching where privacy permits; duplicate research prevention; emergency spending limits.

Use both providers intelligently. Do not unnecessarily duplicate every API request. Preserve the Elite all-inclusive membership model and approved Journey usage policies.

## 9. Owner Intelligence Control Center

Provide centralized owner management for: API provider configuration; service availability; assignment processing; research quality; API expenditures; membership utilization; failed investigations; provider performance; continuous monitoring; member satisfaction; operational profitability.

Protect confidential member research through appropriate access controls.

## 10. Implementation and Testing

Design the integration using our existing Netlify, Supabase and Journey infrastructure. Evaluate asynchronous research processing, background job queues, scheduled monitoring, webhooks, retries and failure recovery.

Test using fictional assignments involving: professional sports franchise ownership; head coach and leadership discovery; business acquisitions; international business expansion; manufacturing and supplier discovery; board and advisory opportunities; retirement and legacy planning.

Confirm commercial usage rights, privacy requirements and API limitations. Add all development requirements to our Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center. Prepare the integrations and provide the exact API credentials and configuration requirements needed from me.

Do not activate paid services or incur new financial commitments until I approve the costs. Maintain all existing Sandbox and commercial launch restrictions.

## Founder Vision

I want Executive and Elite members to experience The Art of Connect as their own private global intelligence department.

We are not selling internet searches.

We are providing structured investigations, opportunity discovery, professional intelligence and meaningful connection assistance through one sophisticated membership experience.
