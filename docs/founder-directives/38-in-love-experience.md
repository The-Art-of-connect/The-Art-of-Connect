THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — In Love Experience

The Singles and Couples experiences must be intuitive, familiar, sophisticated and purpose-driven.

Use eHarmony as a usability reference for Singles, not as a design or intellectual property template. Maintain The Art of Connect’s original branding, curriculum, compatibility methodology and luxury identity.

Singles

Create a recognizable matchmaking experience centered on:

* Compatible member discovery
* Member profiles
* Compatibility insights
* Search preferences
* Expressions of interest
* Mutual connections
* Private messaging
* Identity verification
* Relationship readiness
* Journey guidance

After onboarding, members must be able to locate compatible singles easily without navigating unrelated personal development pages.

Couples

Create a dedicated relationship enrichment experience centered on:

* Shared relationship space
* Partner invitations and acceptance
* Daily relationship check-ins
* Five Love Languages
* Emotional intelligence
* Communication
* Relationship development
* Shared goals
* Shared calendar
* Journey guidance
* Counseling and Wellness resources

Each partner must retain individual privacy and control over information shared within the relationship.

Transition from Singles to Couples

Allow mutually consenting members to pause Singles discovery and transition into Couples while preserving their individual accounts, authorized history and personal development progress.

Simplicity and consistency

Maintain the four-item global navigation: Home, Connect, Journey and My World.

Provide appropriate contextual navigation within Singles and Couples without cluttering the global navigation.

Use existing approved feature names consistently throughout the platform. Do not rename features or establish additional permanent terminology without founder approval.

Restricted membership features must display the appropriate trial, purchase or upgrade pop-up rather than silently redirecting members to Home.

Elite members receive all applicable In Life, In Love and In Business services without additional membership upgrades.

Implementation

Audit the existing Singles and Couples functionality before developing replacements.

Reuse existing approved features and workflows wherever possible. Correct navigation, organization and presentation rather than unnecessarily rebuilding the system.

Provide mobile and desktop previews for founder review.

Include both experiences in the second independent customer experience audit.

Maintain all existing security, privacy, membership eligibility and production activation restrictions.
