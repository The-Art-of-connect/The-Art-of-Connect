THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — Capital & Funding Intelligence Center

Peace,

Expand CONNECT 360 Global Intelligence and our Strategic Business Development Suite to include a comprehensive Capital & Funding Intelligence Center.

I want Journey to help business owners identify appropriate funding opportunities, understand financing requirements, prepare professional funding packages and manage the funding development process.

Our objective is not simply to provide links to lenders. Our objective is to help members become funding-ready.

## 1. Personalized Funding Assessment

Allow Journey to conduct a confidential funding discovery conversation covering: business concept; industry; business structure; startup or acquisition requirements; existing business operations; revenue and profitability; total project costs; available owner capital; existing financial obligations; voluntarily provided credit information; available collateral; funding requirements; repayment capacity; ownership and operational preferences; funding timeline.

Identify missing information before developing a funding strategy.

Do not fabricate financial information or represent self-reported information as verified.

## 2. Capital Strategy Builder

Develop a personalized funding strategy workspace.

Include: total project cost; startup expenses; acquisition expenses; working capital requirements; operating reserves; owner contributions; verified available capital, where authorized; potential financing; confirmed financing; remaining funding requirements; potential funding alternatives; estimated repayment obligations; cash flow considerations; financial risks; development milestones.

Allow members to compare multiple funding scenarios.

Clearly distinguish proposed financing from lender-approved or committed financing.

## 3. Global Funding Intelligence

Utilize Parallel and Exa collaboratively to research relevant funding programs and legitimate financing providers.

Include, where applicable: SBA financing; traditional commercial financing; business lines of credit; equipment financing; commercial real estate financing; franchise financing; seller financing education; community development financing; government funding programs; eligible grants; corporate sponsorships; international business financing; other legitimate funding resources.

Provide educational information about equity financing and referrals to appropriately regulated professionals where authorized.

Research authoritative sources and current financing requirements.

Do not facilitate unauthorized securities offerings or investor solicitation.

## 4. Funding Provider Research

Identify potentially suitable funding providers based on approved research criteria.

Research: provider identity; regulatory or licensing status where applicable; official websites; published financing products; industry restrictions; geographic restrictions; startup eligibility; published credit requirements; collateral requirements; down payment requirements; published interest rates; repayment terms; fees; application processes; official business contact channels.

Provide source links and verification dates.

Explain why each funding option may be relevant.

Do not guarantee funding availability or approval.

## 5. Funding Comparison Reports

Allow Journey to develop professional funding comparison reports.

Include: potentially available loan amounts; intended use of funds; estimated interest expenses; estimated monthly payments; estimated total borrowing costs; fees; collateral considerations; personal guarantee considerations; repayment requirements; potential advantages and disadvantages; applicable eligibility requirements; outstanding information; questions to discuss with lenders and qualified professionals.

Allow members to compare funding options without automatically committing to financing.

## 6. Professional Funding Package Builder

Allow Journey to assist members in preparing comprehensive funding packages.

Generate or help organize: executive summaries; business plans; business development plans; sources and uses of funds; startup budgets; acquisition budgets; revenue projections; expense projections; cash flow projections; projected profit and loss statements; projected balance sheets; break-even analysis; debt repayment analysis; owner experience summaries; market research; risk assessments; funding request letters; supporting document checklists.

Clearly identify assumptions, estimates and information requiring professional verification.

Do not fabricate tax returns, historical financial statements, bank records, professional certifications or proof-of-funds documents.

## 7. Funding Readiness Checklist

Create a dynamic checklist based on the specific financing provider, funding program and business opportunity.

Identify: required documents; documents received; documents awaiting preparation; documents awaiting professional review; missing financial information; unresolved questions; required qualifications; application deadlines; outstanding lender requests.

Distinguish document readiness from financing eligibility and approval.

## 8. Financial Document Vault

Integrate funding documentation with the existing secure Business Deal Room.

Support authorized uploads of: personal financial statements; business financial statements; tax returns; bank statements; proof-of-funds documents; debt schedules; business formation records; purchase agreements; financing applications; lender correspondence; conditional approvals; commitment letters; other applicable supporting documents.

Implement strict role-based access, document permissions, encryption, malware scanning, audit logs and retention controls.

Never transmit confidential financial documents or banking credentials to Parallel or Exa.

## 9. Professional Advisor Assistance

Allow members to invite their own qualified accountants, attorneys and authorized financing professionals into appropriate project-specific workspaces.

Allow advisors to: review authorized financial information; review funding packages; request additional documentation; suggest revisions; upload professional documents; address outstanding financing requirements.

Provide optional referrals to qualified professional service providers.

Disclose any affiliated business relationships, including potential referrals to T. Turner & Associates, LLC.

Do not require members to use an affiliated provider.

Clearly disclose independent professional service fees.

## 10. Funding Communication Center

Allow members to prepare professional communications to legitimate financing providers.

Support: funding inquiries; loan preparation correspondence; requests for financing information; requests for clarification; meeting preparation; application follow-up messages; responses to lender documentation requests.

Require member authorization before external communications are sent.

Provide secure, appropriately authorized document-sharing options.

Do not automatically submit credit applications or disclose financial information without specific authorization and appropriate legal approval.

## 11. Funding Application Tracker

Allow members to track their financing progress.

Support statuses including: researching financing; preparing documentation; ready for professional review; ready to apply; application submitted by member; awaiting lender response; additional documentation requested; under lender review; conditionally approved; approved; declined; withdrawn; funded.

Require supporting information before identifying financing as approved or funded.

Do not automatically change lender decision statuses without reliable authorization or confirmation.

## 12. Funding Improvement Plan

When financing is unavailable or an application is declined, allow Journey to assist the member in understanding the stated obstacles and preparing an improvement plan.

Address potential concerns involving: financial documentation; cash flow; existing debt; available capital; collateral; business planning; operating history; alternative funding requirements; professional assistance.

Provide actionable educational steps.

Do not promise credit score improvements or future funding approval.

## 13. Elite Global Funding Watchlist

Allow Elite members to establish ongoing funding objectives.

Monitor approved sources for: new financing programs; relevant grant opportunities; industry-specific financing; government funding initiatives; international funding resources; changes to published eligibility requirements; relevant application deadlines.

Integrate findings into the Elite Daily Edition and My Intelligence Office.

Allow members to pause, update or discontinue monitoring.

Use configurable research limits and monitoring frequencies.

## 14. Executive and Elite Membership Entitlements

Executive members should receive: funding assessments; capital strategy development; funding research; financing comparisons; professional funding package preparation; document organization; professional advisor collaboration; application tracking.

Elite members should receive all authorized Executive functionality plus: advanced multi-stage funding investigations; multiple concurrent funding projects; advanced financial scenario comparisons; continuous funding monitoring; proactive funding resource discovery; expanded business development support; personalized funding briefings; highest approved intelligence service priority.

Maintain our approved Elite all-inclusive membership model.

## 15. Financial and Regulatory Safeguards

The Art of Connect must not operate as an unauthorized lender, loan broker, securities broker, investment advisor or crowdfunding platform.

Journey must not: guarantee financing; approve or deny credit; misrepresent funding eligibility; fabricate financial records; submit false application information; execute unauthorized financial transactions; collect unauthorized loan placement or transaction fees; facilitate unapproved investment solicitations.

Review applicable lending, credit reporting, privacy, fair lending, brokerage, referral and financial service requirements before activating sensitive production capabilities.

## 16. Owner Management

Create centralized owner controls for: funding resource quality; research providers; funding research costs; member funding assignments; funding package functionality; professional advisor access; application tracking; monitoring expenses; membership usage; fraud reports; regulatory restrictions; service availability.

Protect confidential member financial information.

Do not grant unrestricted administrative access to private financial documents.

## 17. Development and Testing

Integrate the Capital & Funding Intelligence Center with: CONNECT 360; Journey; Parallel; Exa; My Intelligence Office; Business Intelligence Center; Strategic Business Proposal Builder; Business Collaboration Inbox; Business Deal Rooms; Attorney Review Center; Secure Document Vault; Connect Calendar; Elite Daily Edition; Owner Portal.

Reuse existing infrastructure.

Add all requirements to the Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

Test with fictional businesses, fictional lenders and fictional financial documentation.

Maintain Sandbox restrictions.

Do not activate paid external services, regulated financial services or new commercial commitments without owner authorization.

## Founder Vision

I want members to bring their business ideas to The Art of Connect and receive comprehensive assistance with understanding what is required to pursue those ideas.

We should help them discover opportunities, develop professional business plans, identify potential funding resources, prepare appropriate documentation, connect with qualified professionals and organize the steps required to move forward.

Our goal is to help members become prepared, informed and positioned to pursue legitimate business opportunities responsibly.
