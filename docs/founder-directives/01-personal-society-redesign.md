Peace,

We are redesigning The Personal Society onboarding and relationship experience to establish The Art of Connect as a premier intentional connection platform.

The founder personally experienced the original 17-section questionnaire and stopped around Section 7 because the process was too lengthy.

We need meaningful assessments without exhausting members.

1. Phased onboarding

Replace the lengthy continuous questionnaire with independent, short, progressive stages.

Each stage must include:

* Clear purpose.
* Progress indicators.
* Estimated completion time.
* Automatic progress saving.
* Save and continue later.
* Optional questions clearly identified.
* No unnecessary duplicate questions.
* Previously provided answers reused appropriately.
* Accessible mobile, tablet and desktop layouts.

Create a centralized question registry with unique question identifiers, conditional branching, answer reuse, version history and owner-configurable required fields.

Preserve existing member answers where appropriate.

Phase One — Welcome & Authenticity

Collect:

1. Legal name and date of birth.
2. Current city and state.
3. Current relationship status.
4. Dating preferences.
5. Relationship intentions.

Complete appropriate email, identity, age and photograph verification before matchmaking activation.

Require acceptance of applicable legal agreements and community standards.

Singles eligibility is strictly 30+. All other member experiences remain 21+.

Keep legal identity and verification information private.

Phase Two — Understanding You

Ask:

1. What three qualities best describe who you are today?
2. What values are most important to you?
3. What does your ideal lifestyle look like?
4. What are you most proud of becoming?
5. What are you currently working on within yourself?

Use selectable responses and optional written reflections.

Phase Three — Emotional & Relationship Readiness

Ask:

1. When someone disagrees with you, how do you typically respond?
2. When you recognize that you’ve hurt someone, what do you do next?
3. How do you respond when a partner requests personal space?
4. What did your previous relationships teach you about yourself?
5. How do you communicate when your emotional needs aren’t being met?
6. How do you handle feelings of jealousy or insecurity?
7. What does a healthy partnership look like to you?

Develop additional professionally reviewed scenario-based questions as needed.

Do not repeatedly rephrase questions that assess the same information.

Phase Four — Compatibility

Collect only information not previously provided:

* Preferred dating age range.
* Geographic preferences.
* Marriage and long-term intentions.
* Children and family dynamics.
* Lifestyle and travel preferences.
* Optional spiritual beliefs and personal values.
* Financial values and partnership expectations.
* Affection and communication preferences.
* Personal boundaries and relationship dealbreakers.

Do not require income, net worth or unnecessary financial disclosures.

2. Relationship Readiness Profile

Build a private assessment results experience covering self-awareness, communication, accountability, emotional regulation, boundaries and relationship intentions.

Support professionally reviewed scoring rubrics and educational recommendations.

Do not present results as a clinical diagnosis or use unsupported psychological classifications.

When areas for growth are identified:

* Explain the results respectfully.
* Provide educational materials.
* Recommend guided personal development activities.
* Offer opportunities for reflection and reassessment.
* Allow members to choose whether to explore qualified counselors, therapists or coaches.

Do not automatically refer every member with an area for growth to a therapist.

Do not penalize members merely because they receive counseling, disclose a mental health condition or have imperfect assessment results.

Keep educational readiness separate from safety enforcement.

3. Member authenticity and safety

Build a comprehensive Trust & Authenticity system with:

* Identity and age verification.
* Current photograph and appropriate liveness verification.
* Duplicate account and impersonation detection.
* Suspicious activity detection.
* Romance scam and financial solicitation protections.
* Confidential reporting and blocking.
* Human moderation and investigation.
* Account restriction and appeal workflows.
* Security and moderation audit trails.

Restrict matchmaking activation when essential identity verification or safety requirements are unresolved.

Credible evidence of dangerous, fraudulent or prohibited conduct must trigger appropriate safety review.

Do not rely on questionnaire scores alone to determine whether someone is safe or needs clinical treatment.

Do not allow payment or premium membership to override safety restrictions.

4. Intentional dating and The Exclusive Connection

Allow eligible singles to explore multiple mutually accepted connections.

Introduce an Exclusive Connection request when a member chooses to focus exclusively on another member.

Both members must independently and affirmatively accept.

Do not pause matchmaking or close conversations solely because one member requests exclusivity.

Once mutually accepted:

* Pause both active singles matchmaking profiles.
* Stop new dating introductions.
* Respectfully close other active dating conversations.
* Preserve appropriate conversation history and reporting access.
* Notify affected connections that the member is no longer available.
* Do not reveal the identity of the selected partner.
* Offer alternative compatible connections where available.
* Create a shared relationship exploration space.
* Offer first-date planning and shared activities.
* Offer the Couples Experience as an optional membership transition.

Allow either member to independently withdraw from exclusivity, unlink or report a concern.

Do not automatically restore public dating visibility after withdrawal. Require the member’s intentional reactivation.

No automatic couples membership charges.

5. Journey — Personal Connection Concierge

Build a dedicated AI-powered concierge named Journey.

Journey should be warm, insightful, respectful, intelligent, supportive and accountability-oriented.

Journey must support:

* Onboarding guidance.
* Self-discovery and educational explanations.
* Relationship communication assistance.
* First-date ideas and planning.
* Shared interest discovery.
* Romantic experiences.
* Anniversary and special occasion planning.
* Thoughtful gift suggestions.
* Shared goals and relationship activities.
* Personal growth recommendations.
* Optional professional guidance referrals.

Journey should encourage independence, healthy boundaries, reciprocity, emotional awareness and informed decisions.

Journey must never act as a licensed therapist, diagnose members, guarantee relationship outcomes or determine matchmaking safety eligibility without appropriate human oversight.

Never disclose another member’s private answers, journals or communications.

Shared recommendations require appropriate consent from both partners.

Clearly disclose that Journey is an AI assistant.

Require confirmation before any external booking, purchase, message or other consequential action.

6. Membership-based concierge access

Build configurable Journey access levels.

Allow basic educational guidance for standard members.

Provide enhanced personalized assistance for Complete Society, Executive Private and Elite Concierge memberships, according to owner-approved benefits.

Preserve original membership pricing.

Do not promise unlimited human concierge services or external booking capabilities unless they are operational and financially approved.

7. Owner Portal — Journey Intelligence Center

Create a dedicated management section where the owner can:

* Manage Journey’s approved operating instructions.
* Manage company knowledge and brand philosophy.
* Create and edit approved questionnaire content.
* Manage assessment categories and educational scoring rubrics.
* Manage educational responses and recommendations.
* Review and approve content with qualified professionals.
* Configure concierge membership permissions.
* Establish AI usage limits and monitor operating costs.
* Test Journey’s responses before publication.
* Manage version history and rollback.
* Monitor aggregate usage, feedback and safety incidents.
* Disable or restrict individual AI capabilities.

Do not use private founder conversations or confidential member information as AI training material without appropriate authorization and consent.

Protect all credentials and sensitive information.

8. Required testing

Test:

* Duplicate-question prevention.
* Save and resume functionality.
* Account and age eligibility.
* Identity verification restrictions.
* Assessment completion and recommendations.
* Member privacy and access permissions.
* Unilateral and mutual exclusive connection requests.
* Concurrent requests and conflicting commitments.
* Availability notifications.
* Conversation closure and continued safety reporting.
* Independent withdrawal from exclusivity.
* Couples membership transitions.
* Concierge entitlement enforcement.
* AI privacy, safety and unauthorized-action prevention.

Update the Legal & Policy Center, Launch Readiness Center and applicable member agreements.

Build in manageable phases, merge tested functionality into main and deploy without opening public enrollment or enabling live payments.

Clearly identify unavailable features as Coming Soon.

We are not building a platform that merely introduces people. We are building an intentional connection ecosystem that helps people understand themselves, discover meaningful relationships and continue growing after they connect.

Our standard is authenticity, dignity, respect, safety, privacy and genuine value.
