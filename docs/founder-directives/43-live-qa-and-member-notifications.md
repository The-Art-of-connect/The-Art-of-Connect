Founder Directive — Live Q&A Experiences and Member Notifications

Enhance the existing live interaction functionality within The Becoming Circle.

Audit existing functionality before building additional infrastructure.

Initial Schedule

Begin with two live Q&A sessions per day rather than four.

The founder must be able to increase the frequency as membership, participation and operational capacity grow, eventually supporting new sessions every four hours.

Each scheduled session must have a distinct discussion topic and an open Q&A opportunity.

Member Notifications

When a live Q&A begins, eligible online members must receive a branded, dismissible pop-up invitation.

Also deliver an invitation to the member’s Message Center so they can join later.

Each invitation must identify:

* The Becoming Circle
* Live Q&A status
* Current discussion topic
* Host
* Join Live Q&A option
* Maybe later option

Respect membership eligibility and notification preferences. Avoid repeated or intrusive pop-ups.

Continuous Participation

Members must be allowed to join an active session, participate in the discussion or simply listen.

Do not automatically disconnect members from an ongoing session because another scheduled session begins.

Allow members to voluntarily join the new session or remain in their current session while its host continues the discussion.

Support continued listening while navigating other authorized areas of the platform through a persistent audio player.

Owner Portal Controls

Provide centralized management of:

* Daily session frequency
* Scheduled start times and time zones
* Discussion topics
* Assigned hosts and moderators
* Session duration
* Membership eligibility
* Member notifications
* Attendance and participation
* Moderation and reporting
* Operating costs

Allow the founder to adjust the schedule without requiring a new website deployment.

Safety and Testing

Include appropriate moderation, member reporting, privacy protections and clear distinctions between educational discussions and professional counseling.

Include the complete live interaction experience in Journey’s independent customer audit.

Preserve all approved platform terminology, membership restrictions and spending limits.

Do not activate additional paid infrastructure, public enrollment or production services without explicit founder approval.
