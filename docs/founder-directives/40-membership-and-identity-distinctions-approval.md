# 40 — APPROVAL: MEMBERSHIP AND IDENTITY DISTINCTIONS

Status: **implemented**._

Approved, keep both membership and identity distinctions.

The orange checkmark shall represent successful identity verification only, regardless of membership level.

Executive Private membership holders shall be officially identified as Executive Members, using the champagne-gold and charcoal shield emblem.

Elite Concierge membership holders shall be officially identified as Elite Members, using the signature orange-gold and black crown shield emblem.

Replace the previous Elite membership checkmark with the new Elite crown emblem wherever it represented membership rather than identity verification.

Update the Brand Terminology Registry with these approved names.

Display membership emblems only for active memberships and respect existing profile visibility and privacy permissions.

Neither membership emblem shall automatically imply identity, financial or professional credential verification.

Verify that membership downgrades, cancellations and expiration immediately remove the applicable membership distinction without affecting legitimate identity verification.

Include these requirements in the second independent customer experience audit.

Maintain all existing production activation restrictions.
