Founder Directive 48 — Business Deal Rooms: Meeting Minutes and Recording Controls

Every Business Deal Room within CONNECT 360 must include optional meeting documentation and recording capabilities controlled by the authorized meeting host.

1. Host Controls

The host must be able to:

* Start a meeting with or without meeting minutes.
* Enable or disable audio recording independently.
* Start, pause, resume and stop recording.
* Capture decisions and action items.
* Enter manual minutes without recording a conversation.
* Review, edit and approve generated minutes.

Recording and automated transcription must be disabled by default and require informed consent from every participant before beginning. New participants joining an active recording must also consent.

2. Journey-Assisted Meeting Minutes

When authorized, Journey should assist with preparing structured meeting minutes, including:

* Meeting date, time and purpose.
* Participants and their business roles.
* Discussion topics.
* Decisions reached.
* Financial matters discussed.
* Unresolved questions.
* Assigned responsibilities.
* Deadlines and next steps.
* Recommended follow-up meeting topics.

Journey must distinguish proposed arrangements from actual decisions and must never represent discussion minutes as a legally binding agreement.

3. Meeting History

Each Business Deal Room must maintain its own organized meeting history, allowing authorized participants to:

* View approved previous meeting minutes.
* Review outstanding action items.
* Track completed responsibilities.
* Compare project progress across meetings.
* Print or download authorized meeting minutes as a PDF.

Participants added later must not automatically receive access to confidential historical recordings or documents.

4. Security and Confidentiality

Meeting records must remain private to authorized participants, with appropriate access controls, retention settings and audit history.

Recording status must be clearly visible to all participants. Provide a non-recorded meeting option when consent is withheld.

5. Implementation

Audit existing Business Deal Rooms functionality before creating additional infrastructure. Reuse existing secure storage, permissions and Journey capabilities wherever appropriate.

Keep the member experience simple, intuitive and consistent with the luxury design of The Art of Connect.

Do not activate paid recording, transcription or streaming infrastructure without explicit founder approval.

Production services, public enrollment, live payments and production document storage must remain disabled until explicitly authorized.  The record should be able to send out by email or text and it should have a typed copy as well
