Expand CONNECT 360 to ensure members are professionally prepared and appropriately qualified for the opportunities they wish to pursue.

I want The Art of Connect to establish a reputation for introducing credible, qualified and professionally prepared individuals.

## Professional Opportunity Portfolio

Integrate our complimentary Résumé Builder with the existing Opportunity Portfolio.

Support: professional résumés; industry-specific résumés; executive biographies; employment history; education; professional credentials; certifications and licenses; professional references; documented accomplishments; work portfolios; professional photographs; acting and audition reels; business ownership experience; professional objectives; supporting documentation.

Allow members to maintain multiple versions of their résumé for different opportunities.

## Professional Credibility Verification

Clearly distinguish independently verified information, documentation received, self-reported information and outstanding requirements.

Use authorized verification providers and official sources where available.

Never fabricate experience, education, references, credentials or professional accomplishments.

Allow members to correct inaccurate information.

## Opportunity Readiness Engine

Before preparing an application, Journey must compare the opportunity's legitimate requirements against the member's authorized professional portfolio.

Identify: requirements satisfied; requirements requiring verification; missing credentials; missing documentation; relevant experience; application deadlines; required professional materials; outstanding preparation.

Use legitimate role-related requirements rather than wealth, membership status, personality assessments or protected personal characteristics for employment eligibility.

## Personalized Professional Preparation

When requirements are missing, Journey should develop an appropriate preparation plan.

Assist with résumé improvements, cover letters, professional biographies, interview preparation, audition preparation, portfolio organization, educational resources and applicable certification research.

Do not represent uncompleted qualifications as completed.

## Global Opportunity Discovery

Utilize Parallel and Exa to identify legitimate external opportunities through authorized sources.

Include: film and television casting opportunities; professional sports organizations; coaching positions; executive leadership positions; board and advisory opportunities; business ownership opportunities; professional consulting opportunities; speaking engagements; modeling opportunities; business collaborations; government contracting opportunities; international professional opportunities.

Monitor official production company announcements, including Tyler Perry Studios, and other authorized opportunity sources.

Do not guarantee early or exclusive access unless an actual partnership provides it.

## Application Preparation and Submission

When a relevant opportunity is discovered, Journey should:

1. Verify the source and opportunity requirements.
2. Review the member's professional portfolio.
3. Identify outstanding requirements.
4. Prepare an accurate, customized résumé and application package.
5. Present the completed application for member review.
6. Obtain specific submission authorization.
7. Submit through approved integrations where supported.
8. Track application confirmation and responses.
9. Prepare the member for interviews or auditions.
10. Organize follow-up deadlines.

Do not submit applications containing incomplete, fabricated or misleading information.

Where automated submission is not supported, provide the completed application package and official submission instructions.

## Professional Application Center

Create a centralized workspace for résumés, credentials, portfolios, watchlists, applications, interviews, auditions, correspondence and professional development plans.

## Privacy and Safety

Require member consent before transmitting résumés, photographs, professional documents or personal information to external organizations.

Protect confidential documents and professional verification records.

Do not transmit confidential member documentation to external research providers.

Prevent fraudulent opportunities, unauthorized applications and discriminatory screening.
