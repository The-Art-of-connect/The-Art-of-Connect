THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — Strategic Business Development & Professional Collaboration Suite

Peace,

Expand CONNECT 360 Global Intelligence to support the entire strategic business development process, from opportunity discovery through professional collaboration, proposal development, attorney review, document management and ongoing business development.

I do not want our members to simply discover opportunities and receive contact information.

I want Journey to help them develop professional proposals, evaluate collaboration options, identify missing resources, present opportunities to other business owners and coordinate the development process within The Art of Connect.

## 1. Strategic Business Proposal Builder

Develop a professional proposal builder powered by Journey, Parallel and Exa.

Journey must gather relevant information through a guided conversation or structured form.

Prepare comprehensive proposals containing, where applicable: executive summary; opportunity description; supporting research; business objectives; market analysis; business development strategy; financial requirements; proposed contributions; potential ownership structures; collaboration options; management responsibilities; revenue model; financial projections; funding considerations; risk assessment; regulatory considerations; development timeline; legal considerations; exit strategies; outstanding requirements; recommended next steps.

Distinguish factual information from assumptions, estimates and proposed arrangements.

Require the member to review and authorize the proposal before sharing it.

## 2. Proposal Completeness Review

Create a dynamic completeness checklist based on the specific business opportunity.

Journey should: identify missing information; ask relevant follow-up questions; identify unresolved financial considerations; identify missing qualifications; identify required professional assistance; identify documentation requirements; identify inconsistencies; identify outstanding decisions; flag jurisdiction-specific requirements for professional review.

Do not fabricate information.

Allow proposals to remain in draft status while information is being gathered.

Distinguish administrative completeness from legal or financial sufficiency.

## 3. Collaboration Options

Allow Journey to develop and explain potentially applicable collaboration structures.

Examples include: operational partnerships; strategic business collaborations; management arrangements; joint venture concepts; independent ownership with financing; customized business structures.

Explain potential advantages, disadvantages, responsibilities, financial considerations and questions requiring professional review.

Do not automatically select or finalize a structure for the participants.

Keep regulated investment solicitation and securities-related services restricted until appropriately authorized.

## 4. Professional Proposal Presentation

Generate luxury-branded, professional proposals that can be: reviewed within The Art of Connect; saved as drafts; revised; shared with authorized recipients; printed; exported as PDFs; referred to professional advisors.

Maintain version history and identify the author, date and status of each revision.

Clearly label preliminary proposals as nonbinding discussion documents unless appropriately finalized through authorized professional processes.

## 5. Business Collaboration Inbox

Allow members to send approved business proposals through the existing Business Collaboration Inbox.

Recipients must be able to: review proposals; accept an invitation to discuss; decline; request additional information; suggest revisions; ask Journey private questions; invite their attorney; invite their accountant; schedule private meetings; withdraw from discussions; report suspicious or inappropriate proposals.

Acceptance for discussion must not constitute acceptance of a legal agreement or financial commitment.

## 6. Professional Advisor Access

Build secure, project-specific invitation functionality for authorized external professional advisors.

Support: attorneys; accountants; business advisors; financing professionals; other authorized professionals.

Do not require professional advisors to purchase a membership solely to review their client's authorized project materials.

Verify professional identity and credentials where applicable.

Allow each party to invite their own professional advisors.

Do not assume one party's attorney represents all participants.

Provide document-level, participant-level and role-based permissions.

Allow advisors to review, comment, download and upload authorized documents.

Create private advisor spaces accessible only to the appropriate client and their authorized advisors.

Do not automatically share confidential legal advice with other parties.

## 7. Attorney Review Center

Create a designated Attorney Review Center within each Business Deal Room.

Include: documents awaiting review; review requests; attorney comments; requests for additional information; proposed revisions; attorney-uploaded documents; outstanding legal questions; document review status; version history; authorized final documents.

Allow attorneys to identify documents as reviewed, requiring revision or otherwise addressed within their authorized role.

Do not allow Journey to mark documents as legally approved on behalf of an attorney.

## 8. Legal & Business Document Vault

Create a dedicated confidential document vault for each business project.

Support applicable documents such as: strategic business proposals; business development plans; confidentiality agreements; letters of intent; memoranda of understanding; business formation documents; operating agreements; partnership agreements; shareholder agreements; financial statements; proof-of-funds documentation; financing documents; due diligence materials; regulatory approvals; purchase agreements; attorney correspondence; final executed agreements.

Generate project-specific document checklists rather than requiring every document for every business opportunity.

Allow authorized members and advisors to upload documents directly.

Maintain original uploaded documents and version history.

Implement malware scanning, secure storage, access controls, audit logs and appropriate retention policies.

## 9. Journey Document Assistance

With explicit authorization, Journey may assist with: document organization; document classification; summaries; identifying apparent missing information; comparing document versions; preparing questions for attorneys; preparing questions for accountants; identifying referenced deadlines; organizing outstanding tasks.

Journey must not alter original attorney-prepared documents, provide definitive legal opinions, certify legal sufficiency or replace licensed professional advice.

Do not transmit confidential legal or financial documents to external research providers.

Restrict AI analysis of potentially privileged material to appropriately authorized processing arrangements following privacy and legal review.

## 10. Private Business Deal Room

Create an integrated project workspace containing: Opportunity Blueprint; Strategic Business Proposal; collaboration options; authorized participants; private communications; professional advisors; financial planning materials; due diligence; Attorney Review Center; Document Vault; meetings; responsibilities; deadlines; milestones; outstanding requirements; development progress.

Integrate with our existing Global Boardroom, Connect Calendar, Business Intelligence Center and Global Intelligence Division.

## 11. Proposal Revisions and Decisions

Allow authorized participants to suggest revisions and discuss proposed arrangements.

Track: original proposal; revised proposals; requested changes; participant responses; unresolved decisions; professional review status; approved discussion terms; final professionally prepared documents.

Maintain a clear distinction between accepting a discussion, expressing interest, acknowledging a document and entering a legally binding agreement.

Evaluate future electronic signature integration separately.

Do not activate binding execution or ownership-transfer functionality without legal review and owner authorization.

## 12. Ongoing Business Development

Allow participants to continue using their Deal Room after their business relationship has been established.

Support: business development milestones; meeting schedules; responsibilities; operational planning; financial planning; expansion opportunities; professional consultations; document management; progress reviews.

Elite members should receive enhanced ongoing intelligence and proactive resource discovery within approved usage limits.

## 13. Executive & Elite Memberships

Provide Executive members with comprehensive proposal development, business collaboration, professional advisor access and Deal Room functionality.

Provide Elite members with expanded capabilities, including: advanced research depth; multiple concurrent ventures; advanced financial scenario comparisons; ongoing opportunity monitoring; proactive resource discovery; personalized business development briefings; enhanced venture organization; highest approved intelligence service priority.

Preserve our Elite all-inclusive membership policy.

Independent attorney, accountant and other professional service fees are separate and must be appropriately disclosed.

## 14. Privacy, Legal and Financial Safeguards

Preserve our existing Safety Shield and financial privacy requirements.

Do not: disclose confidential information without authorization; automatically transmit private financial records to external providers; represent Journey as a licensed attorney; guarantee business outcomes; guarantee financing; facilitate unauthorized securities offerings; execute unauthorized financial transactions; collect transaction-based compensation without legal approval; expose privileged legal communications to unauthorized participants.

Conduct appropriate legal, privacy and security reviews before activating sensitive production functionality.

## 15. Owner Management and Quality Assurance

Integrate all functionality with our existing Owner Portal, Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

Provide appropriate oversight of: feature availability; proposal processing; professional invitations; document upload functionality; security events; regulatory restrictions; research expenses; membership usage; service profitability; reported issues.

Do not provide unrestricted administrative access to confidential business documents or attorney communications.

Test using fictional organizations, fictional proposals, fictional attorneys and fictional financial information.

Reuse existing infrastructure wherever possible.

Maintain Stripe Sandbox and all existing commercial launch restrictions.

Do not activate new paid services without my authorization.

## Founder Vision

The Art of Connect should be a professional environment where members can discover opportunities, develop their visions, identify resources, establish meaningful collaborations and organize the professional processes required to pursue their objectives.

We want to help our members move beyond ideas and into organized, responsible business development.
