THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Master Directive — The Becoming Circle, Open Discussions & Global Connections

Official Authorization to Begin Development

Peace,

I am ready to move forward with the development of The Becoming Circle and our live open discussion ecosystem.

This directive consolidates our membership, personal development, live conversation, hosting, scheduling, private meeting, privacy and community safety decisions.

Integrate everything into our existing platform. Do not create disconnected or duplicate systems.

This should be a premium, innovative, world-class social experience consistent with our 2027 design standards.

## 1. Our Purpose

The Art of Connect is designed to help individuals: discover who they are; understand who they want to become; identify their personal and professional goals; learn how they communicate and interact with others; discover like-minded individuals; exchange meaningful perspectives; develop friendships, business relationships and romantic relationships; create meaningful experiences together.

Our platform must encourage respectful communication, personal growth, intellectual curiosity and genuine human connection.

## 2. Expand The Complimentary Art of Becoming

The Art of Becoming remains complimentary. Expand the experience beyond discovering who members are today. Include: Who Am I Becoming?; My Future Self; my one-year goals; my five-year goals; my ten-year goals; my personal vision board; My Daily Sanctuary; morning intentions; affirmations and manifestations; daily emotional check-ins; educational life lessons; daily reflection questions; real-life self-discovery scenarios; evening reflections; weekly and monthly progress reviews; private journaling; personal accomplishments; personal growth planning.

Include educational experiences that encourage members to reflect on whether their current relationships, friendships, careers and lifestyle align with the person they are becoming. Help individuals explore whether they and their partners are growing together, communicating effectively and supporting one another's evolving needs. Do not make definitive judgments about whether someone should remain in or leave a relationship. Use approved educational content and prewritten responses for free members without granting access to personalized Journey AI services. With appropriate consent, reuse relevant discovery information within the Personal Discovery Portfolio and Connection Intelligence Engine.

## 3. The Becoming Circle — Complimentary Open Discussions

Create a complimentary interactive community called The Becoming Circle. Provide one newly featured complimentary discussion topic every four hours, resulting in six featured topics every day. Both complimentary and paid members may participate.

Discussion categories should include: everyday life; relationships and marriage; friendships; parenting and children; family dynamics; personal development; business and entrepreneurship; career opportunities; financial mindset; retirement; travel and lifestyle; current events; world perspectives; culture and society; communication and emotional intelligence; personal goals and future ambitions.

Use relevant trending topics as inspiration, but avoid sensationalism, gossip, humiliation, misinformation and unnecessary controversy. Do not automatically reproduce other creators' content. Allow members to remain in their current discussion when a new featured topic becomes available, provided appropriate moderation remains available. Free members receive one newly featured general discussion every four hours, not multiple additional exclusive discussion rooms. This is an intentional exception to our previous paid-only social interaction restrictions.

## 4. Complimentary Member Participation

Free members may: enter the complimentary Becoming Circle; listen to moderated live conversations; read and submit written comments; ask questions; participate in polls; share their perspectives through text; suggest future topics; view upcoming discussion topics; purchase an individual speaking turn.

Free members must be able to experience meaningful human connection before deciding to purchase a membership. However, reserve deeper networking, personalized discussion lounges, individual connections, private communications and advanced social experiences for applicable paid memberships.

## 5. Paid Member Discussion Lounges

Paid members receive access to the complimentary Becoming Circle plus additional specialized discussion lounges according to their memberships. Create multiple simultaneous discussions tailored to: personality preferences; communication styles; personal values; interests; work styles; lifestyle preferences; relationship intentions; current life stages; personal and professional ambitions; future goals.

Use authorized information from the Personal Discovery Portfolio and Connection Intelligence Engine. We cannot meaningfully recommend compatible people or groups without first understanding the individual. Do not recommend personalized compatibility when insufficient relevant information exists. Encourage members to complete their self-discovery activities. Do not require every individual to finish the entire curriculum before purchasing a membership. Do not permanently assign members to groups based on personality. Allow members to independently explore other eligible discussion rooms. Maintain applicable membership, age, relationship status and Elite qualification requirements.

## 6. Mandatory Live Human Moderation

Every active live open discussion must have an authorized human moderator. This applies to complimentary discussions, paid discussion lounges, member-hosted public discussions and other open live conversation experiences. No open live audio discussion may operate without an authorized moderator. If a moderator is unavailable, keep the discussion in text-only mode, reschedule the live session or display the scheduled live start time. If a moderator disconnects unexpectedly, pause live speaking privileges until an authorized moderator resumes control.

Moderators must be able to: open the discussion; introduce the topic; explain the community standards; manage speaking requests; approve speakers; control the speaking queue; start and end speaking turns; mute microphones; manage audience comments; ask follow-up questions; read relevant written perspectives aloud; create polls; issue warnings; remove disruptive participants; pause the conversation; close the discussion; escalate safety concerns.

Provide backup moderator assignment capabilities. Record moderator actions in an appropriate audit history.

## 7. Official Two-Minute Speaking Rule

Every participant receives a maximum standard speaking allowance of two minutes per turn in open discussions. Implement a server-controlled real-time countdown timer. The timer begins when the participant's speaking turn is activated and their microphone is available.

Provide: a visible two-minute countdown; an orange warning when 30 seconds remain; a gentle notification when 10 seconds remain; automatic microphone muting when the time expires; moderator intervention when necessary.

Each active speaker must have a prominent button labeled: I'm Finished Speaking.

When the speaker selects this button: 1. Immediately end their speaking turn. 2. Mute their microphone. 3. Mark their turn as completed. 4. Notify the moderator and update the speaking queue. 5. Allow the next authorized participant to begin speaking. 6. Return the completed speaker to audience participation. 7. Display an option to raise their hand again.

Do not require someone to use their entire two minutes if they have finished speaking. Participants may return to the queue multiple times throughout the same discussion. Returning speakers must wait for another appropriate opportunity to speak. Give reasonable priority to individuals who have not yet spoken. Allow moderators to extend speaking time when necessary, with appropriate accountability. Only one participant should have an active speaking microphone at a time, excluding authorized moderator intervention. Do not allow microphone interruptions, talking over other members or dominating the conversation.

## 8. Speaking Privileges and Purchases

Eligible paid members receive speaking privileges as part of their memberships. They may request additional speaking turns without purchasing additional speaking access, subject to fair queue management. Complimentary members may purchase individual two-minute speaking turns. Provide the technical ability to offer speaking passes if approved by the owner. All speaking prices must be configurable.

Previously discussed proposed prices: individual speaking turn: $2.99; speaking day pass: $7.99. These prices are not final and require owner approval before activation.

Only sell speaking access when appropriate moderated speaking capacity is available. Provide clear refund or credit procedures if the platform cannot deliver a purchased speaking opportunity. Purchasing a speaking turn does not provide priority over other participants. Preserve our Elite membership benefit structure: included platform services must not incur additional feature charges. Additional Journey usage beyond the included allowance remains separately chargeable.

## 9. Create the Conversation — Membership Privileges

Allow paid members to create and submit their own discussion topics. Apply privileges according to their membership level.

One Society Membership: submit a proposed conversation topic; select an applicable category; receive an assigned date and time following approval; receive notification when the conversation is scheduled; receive a shareable invitation for eligible open discussions.

Two Society Memberships: submit a proposed conversation topic; select an applicable category; select from available dates and times; receive an assigned moderator; receive a shareable invitation.

All Three Society Memberships: submit a conversation topic; select an available date and time; choose whether to request a platform moderator or moderate the discussion personally; personally moderate after completing required training and authorization; open and close the discussion; manage the speaking queue; guide the conversation; share invitations.

Executive Private: provide priority scheduling and the ability to create public, approval-required or closed private meetings.

Elite Concierge: provide the highest scheduling priority, advanced hosting privileges and access to the full private meeting experience.

## 10. Automated Topic Approval

The system must review submitted conversation topics and determine whether to approve or decline them. Consider: community standards; membership privileges; topic relevance; threats and harassment; inappropriate or discriminatory content; fraudulent promotions; privacy concerns; safety risks; scheduling availability; moderator availability; room capacity. Provide clear explanations for declined topics. Allow members to revise and resubmit appropriate topics. Provide authorized human review for appeals, uncertain decisions and exceptional circumstances. The owner must not be required to manually approve every routine topic submission.

## 11. Membership Scheduling Priority

Implement the following scheduling hierarchy: 1. Elite Concierge. 2. Executive Private. 3. All Three Societies. 4. Two Societies. 5. One Society.

Elite members receive the highest priority over pending lower-tier requests. Executive Private members receive the next highest priority. Within each membership level, prioritize requests according to submission date and requested availability. Allow approved topics to be scheduled days or weeks in advance depending on availability. Notify members of their confirmed date and time. Protect already-confirmed reservations from unexpected displacement unless exceptional circumstances require rescheduling. Prevent indefinite delays for standard members through appropriate capacity management. Scheduling priority does not override personal boundaries, moderation standards or fair speaking queues.

## 12. Member Moderator Training

Create an owner-approved educational training experience called The Art of Leading a Conversation. Eligible member hosts must complete moderator training before personally moderating live open discussions. Training should include: respectful communication; managing different personalities; encouraging participation; handling disagreements; remaining fair and impartial; managing speaking turns; enforcing the two-minute rule; recognizing harassment and threats; protecting personal information; preventing inappropriate solicitations; escalating safety concerns. Maintain moderator authorization, training completion and accountability records.

## 13. Public, Approval-Required and Private Meetings

Create an Executive and Elite exclusive experience called The Private Meeting Suite. Allow eligible members to select: Open Member Meeting — other eligible members may discover and join the meeting if interested in the subject. Approval-Required Meeting — interested members may request access; the meeting creator may accept or decline their request. Closed Private Meeting — only specifically invited and authorized participants may enter.

Allow Executive and Elite members to create private international business discussions, collaboration meetings, professional meetings and other appropriate gatherings. Allow them to choose their meeting topic, select available times and invite specific participants. Private meeting hosts must have appropriate participant and safety controls. Allow private meeting hosts to determine whether timed speaking turns are necessary. The mandatory two-minute speaking rule applies to open discussion rooms. Public discussions and closed private meetings must be capable of operating simultaneously, subject to approved technical capacity.

## 14. Private Meeting Suite and Global Boardroom

Plan the professional meeting experience to support: audio meetings; video meetings; screen sharing; meeting agendas; secure meeting chat; document sharing; virtual waiting rooms; participant permissions; meeting notes; follow-up action items; recurring meetings; international time zones; calendar invitations; permanent invitation-only business collaboration groups. Implement these capabilities in approved development phases. Do not commit to additional paid infrastructure without owner approval.

## 15. Invitations and External Registration

Provide approved open conversation creators with: a unique shareable registration link; a branded promotional invitation; a QR code; a shareable social media description; calendar invitation capabilities. Allow members to promote their approved discussions through social media, email and other appropriate external channels. Individuals who are not members may register for a complimentary account through an eligible invitation. Provide appropriate access to the specific invited discussion without unlocking unrelated paid features. For private meetings, use secure, individually authorized and revocable invitations. Allow Executive and Elite members to select existing eligible members whom they wish to invite. Recipients must be able to accept or decline. Do not automatically enroll invited participants in paid memberships. Track invitation registrations, membership trials and paid conversions within the Owner Analytics Center.

## 16. Follow, Connect and Continue the Conversation

Allow members to discover individuals through meaningful discussions. Provide eligible members with the ability to: follow a member; request a connection; appreciate a perspective; request to continue a conversation privately; invite someone to an eligible discussion or meeting. Following a member does not automatically authorize private communication. The receiving member must be able to accept, decline, ignore or block connection requests. Allow connection purposes to include: business; professional networking; friendship; mentorship; travel; personal growth; romantic interest for eligible singles; couples connections. Do not automatically interpret business or friendship interactions as romantic interest. Respect the recipient's communication preferences and consent.

## 17. The Art of Connect Safety Shield

Implement comprehensive personal information and communication protections. Detect and prevent inappropriate sharing of sensitive information, including: personal telephone numbers; residential addresses; personal email addresses; Social Security numbers; bank account information; card information; passwords; login credentials; children's identifying information; other sensitive personal information. Apply protections to written comments, private messages, profiles, conversation submissions, meeting chats and uploaded content where technically feasible. When potentially private contact information is detected, prevent delivery or publication and display a respectful privacy notice. Ask whether the information is personal or legitimate publicly available business or event information. Do not automatically permit restricted information merely because a member labels it as business information. Provide appropriate verification or review procedures. Allow approved public business websites, business locations and official event information where appropriate. Maintain authorized exceptions for secure registration, identity verification, billing and administrative processes.

## 18. Community Communication Standards

Actively protect members against: threats; harassment; bullying; personal attacks; talking down to other members; discriminatory remarks; humiliation; verbal fights; unwanted advances; fraudulent business solicitations; romance scams; identity impersonation; extortion; unauthorized personal information sharing. Use contextual screening rather than relying exclusively on individual prohibited words. Encourage members to revise disrespectful responses before publication. Block serious violations and escalate appropriate incidents. Allow reporting, blocking, warnings, restrictions, suspensions and appeals. Human moderators must intervene immediately when inappropriate behavior occurs during live discussions. Membership status never overrides community standards, consent or safety.

## 19. Member Privacy and Monitoring

Create a My Privacy & Safety Center. Allow members to control: who follows them; who sends connection requests; who invites them to meetings; who can see their profile; online visibility; public activity visibility; blocked accounts; notification preferences. Disclose applicable safety monitoring practices. Do not create unrestricted staff access to private member communications. Restrict and audit access to flagged private communications. Keep personal discovery answers, private journals, counseling information and Elite verification information protected from social visibility. Do not record or replay private meetings or live audio without appropriate disclosures, authorization and consent.

## 20. Personalized Connection Calendar

Create a personalized conversation calendar for paid members. Recommend discussions according to authorized discovery information, interests, goals, communication preferences and current life stages. Allow members to: follow conversation categories; follow approved hosts; follow recurring conversation series; save upcoming discussions; receive optional reminders; view scheduled times in their local time zone; discover relevant opportunities. Integrate with the existing Connect Calendar. Allow approved recurring conversation series with owner-configurable scheduling privileges.

## 21. Complimentary Membership Preview

Do not hide the broader platform from complimentary members. Display locked paid experiences in an elegant greyed-out design. Allow free members to view appropriate previews of: additional discussion rooms; upcoming conversation topics; business networking; friendship communities; specialized social circles; singles and couples experiences; private meeting benefits; Journey services; Executive and Elite opportunities. When a free member selects a locked feature, explain the applicable membership benefits and provide an option to purchase membership or begin an eligible 15-day trial. Do not expose private conversations, sensitive member information or unauthorized content. Never fabricate participation, attendance or compatible connections.

## 22. Owner Global Conversation Control Center

Create a comprehensive Owner Portal management experience. Allow the owner to oversee: complimentary four-hour topic rotations; paid conversation lounges; submitted topics; approved and declined topics; scheduling queues; membership priorities; active public discussions; active private meeting status; moderator availability; moderator training and permissions; speaking queues; speaking durations; speaking fees and purchases; refunds and credits; guest registrations; member invitations; trial and paid membership conversions; reports and appeals; community violations; room capacity; infrastructure expenses; moderator expenses; operating performance. Provide emergency controls to pause or close an unsafe open discussion. Automate routine operations so the owner does not have to personally manage every discussion. Do not expose confidential meeting content through routine owner monitoring.

## 23. Technology, Accessibility and Operating Costs

Evaluate appropriate real-time communication infrastructure. Live member-to-member audio and video must not be confused with Journey's existing personalized AI voice service. Evaluate: concurrent room capacity; concurrent listeners and speakers; real-time speaking queues; reliable countdown timers; microphone controls; moderator permissions; secure private meetings; accessibility; live captions; international communication; multilingual captions and translation; bandwidth expenses; human moderation expenses; storage and security expenses. Begin with an audio-first open discussion experience. Provide written participation alternatives for members who cannot or prefer not to speak. Present new infrastructure options, estimated costs and operational requirements to the owner before purchasing services or making financial commitments.

## 24. Development Phases

Phase One: Build the Becoming Circle foundation, four-hour topic scheduling, free and paid access, written discussions, moderator management, safety protections and membership previews.

Phase Two: Implement moderated live audio, two-minute speaking turns, the I'm Finished Speaking control, microphone management, speaking queues and sandbox speaking purchases.

Phase Three: Implement personalized paid discussion lounges, member-created topics, membership scheduling priorities, moderator training and external invitations.

Phase Four: Implement Executive and Elite private meeting capabilities, public and private meeting selections, international scheduling and advanced business collaboration.

Phase Five: Implement approved advanced features, multilingual communication, recurring conversation series, additional meeting tools and expanded analytics.

Add each feature to the Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center. Reuse existing functionality wherever possible.

## 25. Required Quality Assurance

Test: complimentary discussion permissions; four-hour topic rotation; existing discussion continuation; paid room restrictions; personalized recommendations; moderator authorization; moderator disconnection; two-minute countdown accuracy; early completion of a speaking turn; automatic microphone muting; transition to the next speaker; rejoining the speaking queue; simultaneous speaking requests; free speaking purchases; paid speaking privileges; refund and credit procedures; membership scheduling priorities; topic approval and appeals; public and private meeting permissions; invitation security; expired membership access; personal information blocking; threat and harassment detection; reporting and blocking; concurrent room functionality; mobile responsiveness; accessibility; privacy and database security.

Provide direct testing links and clearly identify which features are operational, pending or awaiting external infrastructure. Maintain all existing commercial launch restrictions. Keep payments in Stripe Sandbox until explicitly authorized. Do not activate public commercial enrollment, live charges or unapproved services.

Every participant deserves an opportunity to be heard.

Every participant deserves respect.

We are creating a platform that allows people to learn about themselves, connect with others, share their voices, develop relationships and conduct business around the world.
