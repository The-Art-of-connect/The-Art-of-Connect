Built into the existing Résumé Builder (no duplicate system). Peace,

Make a current professional résumé on file a mandatory requirement for every Executive and Elite member as part of their professional onboarding.

Members must have three options:

1. Upload an existing résumé.
2. Build a new résumé with Journey.
3. Review and approve a résumé already saved in their account.

Support PDF and Word uploads, secure document processing and extraction of professional information.

When a member uploads an existing résumé, Journey should prepare an enhanced professional draft.

Journey should improve formatting, grammar, professional summaries, accomplishment descriptions, industry-specific terminology and ATS compatibility.

The objective is to present our members professionally and position them for meaningful consideration when opportunities become available.

Never fabricate qualifications, employment history, education, credentials or accomplishments.

Ask members questions when information is missing or requires clarification.

Preserve the original résumé, maintain revision history and require member approval of the enhanced version.

Allow members to maintain multiple customized résumés for different professional opportunities while preserving one accurate master professional history.

Integrate résumé information with the Professional Opportunity Portfolio, Opportunity Readiness Engine, CONNECT 360, Parallel, Exa and the Professional Application Center.

Keep confidential résumé documents and personal identifiers out of external research requests.

When an opportunity is discovered, Journey should prepare a customized résumé, cover letter, professional introduction and applicable supporting materials for member approval.

Create a Professional Readiness Dashboard showing completed and outstanding portfolio requirements.

Require an approved résumé on file before initiating professional introductions or submitting employment applications on behalf of Executive and Elite members.

Do not block access to unrelated social, educational or personal membership services while a résumé is being completed.

Prompt members periodically to review and update their professional information.

Complimentary members retain access to our free Résumé Builder using guided forms and templates without personalized Journey AI.

Maintain our professional luxury design standards throughout this experience.

I want The Art of Connect to become known for presenting credible, professionally prepared individuals who are ready to pursue the opportunities they seek.

Integrate this enhancement into the existing development plan. Do not create a duplicate résumé system or activate unapproved paid services.
