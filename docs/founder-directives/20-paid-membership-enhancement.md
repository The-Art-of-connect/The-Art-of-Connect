THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Master Directive — Paid Membership Enhancement, Executive Private & Elite Concierge

Authorization to Enhance the Paid Membership Experience

Peace,

We are ready to significantly enhance our paid membership ecosystem.

Our complimentary membership should provide meaningful self-discovery, educational content and introductory community participation.

However, our paid memberships must offer substantially greater value, deeper connections, personalized experiences and compelling reasons for members to remain subscribed.

Executive Private and Elite Concierge must provide distinctly elevated experiences.

Do not simply add features to a standard dashboard. Create an intentional progression of membership value, design, convenience, privacy, opportunities and privileges.

Integrate these enhancements with our existing platform and previously approved Founder Master Directives.

Do not replace working functionality or build duplicate systems.

## 1. Membership Philosophy

Our membership progression is: Complimentary: Discover Yourself. · One Society: Discover Your People. · Two Societies: Expand Your World. · Complete Society: Experience Life Across All Three Societies. · Executive Private: Exceptional Access and Private Experiences. · Elite Concierge: Our Highest Level of Membership Service.

Retain existing approved membership pricing and entitlement rules. Do not change membership pricing without owner authorization. Preserve eligible 15-day membership trials and all commercial launch restrictions.

## 2. Discover The Art of Connect — Website Feature Directory

Create a prominent section titled: What Does This Website Offer?

This should be a comprehensive, searchable, expandable feature directory accessible throughout the website.

Do not create another AI concierge service. Journey already provides personalized AI assistance to eligible paid members. The feature directory should operate without unnecessary AI usage.

Each expandable feature description must explain: what the feature is; what it provides; how it benefits the member; how to use it; whether it is included in their current membership; applicable eligibility requirements; a direct link to the feature; available upgrade options when applicable.

Include an Expand All and Collapse All option.

Organize the directory into appropriate categories, including: personal development; Journey AI; communities; live conversations; individual connections; professional networking; business opportunities; friendships; singles; couples; travel; events and experiences; educational webinars; counseling and wellness; private meetings; executive services; elite services; privacy and safety; membership management.

Free members must be able to expand descriptions of paid features while actual paid functionality remains restricted. Use elegant greyed-out states and lock indicators for unavailable features. Allow paid members to distinguish between included services and services available through an upgrade.

Create a What's New at The Art of Connect section.

Maintain the directory through a central feature registry connected to actual membership permissions and release status.

Do not advertise unfinished functionality as operational.

## 3. Paid Member Homepage

Create an engaging paid-member dashboard providing direct access to: current and upcoming conversations; relevant communities; new connection opportunities; upcoming events; member-created discussions; accepted connection requests; private messages; personalized Journey assistance; personal goals and progress; membership benefits; recently released features.

Use authorized discovery information and preferences to recommend relevant experiences. Provide clear navigation for members who prefer to explore independently. Do not fabricate participation, recommendations, opportunities or member activity.

## 4. Personalized Journey Experience

Journey must remain an exclusive paid membership benefit. Provide personalized text and live voice assistance throughout authorized experiences.

Journey should be able to assist with: navigating the website; understanding membership features; personal development; goal planning; emotional intelligence education; communication practice; relationship education; business brainstorming; professional growth; preparing for conversations; exploring eligible events; understanding community recommendations; preparing connection requests; reflecting on personal progress.

Journey may explain why a particular community or connection may be relevant when supported by sufficient authorized information. Never guarantee compatibility or represent AI-generated guidance as licensed professional advice. Do not send messages, create commitments or disclose private information without appropriate member authorization. Maintain membership usage allowances and approved additional usage pricing.

## 5. Connect Me With Someone Who

Create an intentional connection discovery experience. Allow eligible paid members to describe the type of connection they wish to establish.

Examples include: someone who enjoys international travel; someone who shares my lifestyle preferences; someone who understands business ownership; someone who has achieved goals similar to mine; someone who enjoys meaningful conversations; someone who shares my relationship values; someone who is preparing for retirement; someone who is starting a new chapter; couples with compatible interests; potential business collaborators; mentors and professional peers.

Use sufficient authorized discovery information, preferences, connection intentions and eligibility rules. Provide explanations of relevant shared or complementary qualities. Only recommend individuals who have authorized the applicable discovery and introduction experience. Do not expose confidential discovery answers. Do not classify financial success or membership status as a measure of human worth. Require mutual consent before establishing deeper individual communication.

## 6. Find People Like Me and Find Your Circle

Enhance our existing connection discovery systems. Consider relevant information such as: personality preferences; communication styles; work styles; personal values; interests; social preferences; lifestyle preferences; current life stages; relationship intentions; personal ambitions; professional goals; one-, five- and ten-year visions.

Use shared and complementary qualities where appropriate. Do not force members into assigned communities. Allow eligible members to explore communities beyond their recommended selections. Use discovery completeness requirements before generating personalized compatibility recommendations.

## 7. The Connection Table

Create smaller, personalized group conversation experiences for paid members.

Potential Connection Tables include: The Friendship Table; The Entrepreneur's Table; The Travel Table; The Singles Table; The Couples Table; The Personal Growth Table; The Next Chapter Table; The Retirement Table; The Executive Roundtable; The Elite Table.

Recommend suitable participants using authorized preferences. Allow members to accept or decline invitations. Provide configurable group sizes, schedules and moderator assignments. Every open live table must have an authorized human moderator. Apply the approved two-minute speaking rule where applicable. These experiences should encourage deeper interaction without requiring members to participate in large open discussion rooms.

## 8. Professional Society Enhancements

Expand The Professional Society into a comprehensive business and professional development experience. Include:

Business Idea Lab — Allow members to explore business concepts based on their interests, experience, goals, strengths and available resources. Provide educational guidance and personalized Journey assistance.

Entrepreneur's Roundtable — Create constructive discussions for aspiring and established business owners.

Business Problem-Solving Rooms — Allow members to exchange perspectives on legitimate business challenges.

Business Collaborator Discovery — Help members discover potential collaborators based on mutually authorized professional interests.

Mentorship Exchange — Allow members to seek or volunteer mentorship.

Business Accountability Connections — Allow consenting members to support each other's progress toward professional goals.

Global Business Connections — Provide opportunities to connect with professionals internationally.

Knowledge Exchange — Preserve and enhance educational webinars, member-hosted learning opportunities and professional discussions.

Business Resource Library — Provide useful educational resources, planning guides, checklists and templates.

Business Growth Planning — Integrate professional goals with My Future Self, Journey and relevant community opportunities.

Do not create a spam-based lead-generation system. Prohibit fraudulent business offers, misleading investment promises, predatory solicitation and unauthorized fundraising.

## 9. Personal Society — Singles and Social Connections

The Personal Society must offer value beyond romantic matchmaking. Include applicable experiences for: intentional dating; meaningful friendships; social circles; dinner and brunch experiences; travel companions; concert and entertainment companions; cultural experiences; lifestyle communities; personal growth conversations; new life chapters; shared-interest activities; retirement friendships.

Preserve established singles eligibility requirements. Do not automatically interpret friendship, travel or professional intentions as romantic interest. Require mutual consent for romantic introductions and private communication. When a member enters a relationship, provide appropriate membership transition options without forcing them to lose their established friendships or personal development history.

## 10. Personal Society — Couples

Develop a complete experience for couples who want to grow individually and together. Include: Our Shared Future; shared one-, five- and ten-year goals; weekly relationship reflections; communication education; love language education; Understanding My Partner; shared vision boards; couples activities; shared calendars; date-night planning; couples travel communities; couples friendships; couples discussion tables; personalized Journey educational assistance.

Allow both partners to independently complete reflection questions and voluntarily share selected answers. Preserve each individual's private account, journal, discovery answers and personal boundaries. Do not automatically disclose private answers to the other partner. Require mutual consent for linked couple experiences. Do not represent educational relationship guidance as professional counseling.

## 11. Personal Growth for Paid Members

Enhance complimentary personal development with personalized paid services. Provide eligible members with: personalized growth plans; goal development assistance; practical action steps; weekly goal reviews; monthly progress reflections; future self planning; communication exercises; personal accountability opportunities; relevant community recommendations; educational activities tailored to their chosen goals.

Integrate with the existing Art of Becoming, Art of Self, Daily Sanctuary, Discovery Portfolio and My Future Self. Do not recreate these systems.

## 12. Member Social Experiences

Enhance the existing social ecosystem. Include: Connection Feed; Connection Moments; Connection Circles; Connection Experiences; member profiles; follow capabilities; mutual connections; private messages; group conversations; event discovery; shared interests; community accomplishments; member-created discussions.

Allow members to continue meaningful conversations outside of discussion rooms through mutually accepted in-platform connections. Respect member intentions, privacy preferences and communication restrictions. Do not permit unsolicited private communication solely because someone follows another member.

## 13. Live Conversation Privileges

Preserve all previously approved Becoming Circle requirements. Complimentary members receive one new general discussion topic every four hours.

Eligible paid members receive: additional simultaneous discussion rooms; personalized conversation recommendations; included speaking privileges; member-generated discussion topics; scheduling privileges according to membership; approved hosting privileges according to membership; deeper connection opportunities.

Maintain mandatory human moderation for open live discussions. Maintain two-minute speaking turns, early completion through I'm Finished Speaking, automatic muting and the ability to rejoin the queue. Membership scheduling priority must not override respectful speaking queue management.

## 14. Travel and Shared Experiences

Create a meaningful travel and social discovery experience. Allow eligible paid members to: explore destination-based communities; discover compatible potential travel companions; discuss travel preferences; share travel experiences; exchange public travel recommendations; create eligible group travel planning spaces; discover upcoming experiences; invite consenting members to activities.

Do not guarantee the trustworthiness of travel companions. Do not automatically share residential addresses, personal telephone numbers or sensitive travel information. Provide appropriate travel safety guidance. The platform must not represent itself as the organizer of third-party travel experiences unless specifically authorized.

## 15. Personalized Connect Calendar

Enhance the existing Connect Calendar. Include: upcoming live discussions; member-created conversations; educational webinars; social experiences; travel discussions; business meetings; couples activities; personalized recommendations; saved events; accepted invitations; exclusive Executive and Elite experiences.

Allow members to follow topics, approved hosts and recurring conversation series. Provide optional reminders and local time-zone display. Do not automatically commit members to attending events.

## 16. Weekly Connection Report

Create an optional personalized weekly membership summary. Include appropriate information about: conversations attended; new mutually accepted connections; upcoming discussions; relevant experiences; personal goal progress; saved events; membership features not yet explored; suggested next steps.

Do not use manipulative engagement techniques or public popularity rankings. Provide notification and frequency preferences. Use deterministic summaries wherever appropriate to control AI expenses.

## 17. Executive Private — Distinct Premium Experience

Create a dedicated Executive Private dashboard. The Executive experience must emphasize: time; privacy; convenience; professional relationships; leadership; international collaboration; meaningful personal experiences.

Create exclusive or enhanced services including:

Executive Lounge — Executive-level professional and social discussions.

Executive Roundtables — Smaller discussions focused on leadership, professional growth, collaboration and lifestyle.

Executive Introductions — Personalized introductions to consenting individuals with relevant interests and objectives.

Executive Private Meeting Suite — Allow members to create public, approval-required and closed private meetings. Provide participant selection, invitations, host controls and applicable scheduling privileges.

Global Business Boardroom — Provide secure international collaboration capabilities through approved development phases.

Executive Collaboration Groups — Allow eligible members to establish ongoing invitation-only professional communities.

Executive Opportunity Exchange — Provide moderated opportunities to explore collaboration, mentorship and professional interests.

Executive Calendar — Provide centralized access to relevant meetings, discussions and experiences.

Executive Privacy Mode — Provide advanced visibility controls, invitation preferences, availability settings and profile discretion.

Executive Priority Scheduling — Place Executive members ahead of standard society memberships for pending scheduling requests. Elite members retain the highest scheduling priority.

Executive Member Support — Provide a dedicated priority support experience, subject to approved staffing and service standards. Do not promise continuous human concierge availability unless appropriate staffing exists.

## 18. Elite Concierge — Our Highest Membership Experience

Elite Concierge must feel substantially different from every other membership. Create an elegant, distinguished, private environment consistent with our luxury branding. Elite members should experience exceptional convenience, personalized opportunities and the highest level of included platform privileges. Retain approved Elite qualification, identity verification and owner authorization requirements. Do not allow individuals to purchase verified Elite status without satisfying eligibility requirements.

## 19. Elite Dashboard and Design

Develop a dedicated Elite homepage. Include: signature luxury visual design; verified Elite membership badge; personalized welcome; Elite Inner Circle; My Introductions; My Elite Calendar; My Private Meetings; Global Elite Boardroom; Elite Opportunity Exchange; Elite Travel Society; Elite Personal Connections; Elite Legacy Circle; My Journey; My Privacy; Elite Member Support.

Use our established luxury branding, including black, rich espresso, burgundy, elegant gold and appropriate orange accents. Maintain the approved Elite verification badge design. Carry the premium design throughout Elite invitations, notifications, emails, meetings and membership communications. Do not create misleading displays of wealth or accomplishment.

## 20. The Elite Inner Circle

Create a verified Elite-only community. Allow eligible members to discover peers according to: professional interests; personal interests; lifestyle preferences; personal and professional ambitions; philanthropic interests; travel preferences; shared experiences; relationship intentions where applicable.

Allow Elite members to voluntarily participate in broader eligible communities. Do not automatically restrict Elite members to interacting only with other Elite members. Preserve their ability to select Elite-only introductions when desired.

## 21. Elite Bespoke Introductions

Create an advanced introduction experience for Elite members. Allow members to specify their desired connection purpose. Examples: business collaboration; international professional connections; mentorship; friendship; travel; philanthropy; shared lifestyle experiences; romantic relationships for eligible singles; couples friendships.

Provide highly relevant introductions based on sufficient authorized information. Require appropriate consent and eligibility. Allow members to accept, decline or refine introduction preferences. Never disclose private financial verification records or confidential personal discovery information.

## 22. Elite Global Boardroom

Provide the highest available business collaboration privileges. Support approved capabilities for: private business meetings; international discussions; virtual meeting rooms; secure meeting invitations; participant approval; meeting agendas; screen sharing; secure document sharing; meeting notes; action items; recurring meetings; ongoing private collaboration groups; international time-zone coordination.

Allow public and private meetings to operate independently and simultaneously, subject to technical capacity. Private meetings must not be exposed through public community feeds. Do not enable recording without appropriate disclosure and participant authorization.

## 23. Elite Opportunity Exchange

Create a carefully moderated Elite opportunity experience. Allow members to voluntarily express interests in: business collaboration; international expansion; professional partnerships; mentorship; philanthropic projects; community initiatives; innovation; leadership; educational opportunities.

Do not operate an unauthorized securities marketplace or investment exchange. Do not guarantee opportunities, business success or financial returns. Maintain strong fraud prevention and reporting controls.

## 24. Elite Travel Society

Create exclusive travel-oriented communities for verified Elite members. Include: international travel discussions; luxury travel preferences; destination communities; cultural experiences; travel companionship discovery; couples travel experiences; shared itineraries; private travel planning groups; relevant social experiences.

Allow members to voluntarily explore broader travel communities. Do not charge additional platform access fees for included Elite travel features. Clearly disclose any independent travel provider charges.

## 25. Elite Signature Social Experiences

Provide exclusive opportunities to discover or create appropriate experiences, including: Elite dinners; private social discussions; leadership gatherings; cultural experiences; international networking; couples gatherings; philanthropic events; lifestyle experiences; educational roundtables.

Display only genuine, available or properly scheduled experiences. Do not fabricate exclusive events or imply that the platform is hosting an event when it is not.

## 26. Elite Legacy Circle

Create an exclusive discussion and educational community focused on long-term vision. Topics may include: personal legacy; retirement; business succession; generational goals; financial education; philanthropy; family relationships; life after professional success; mentoring future generations; meaningful experiences.

Use qualified professional resources where regulated financial, legal or tax advice is required. Do not treat Elite membership as proof of professional expertise.

## 27. Elite Relationship and Couples Experiences

Provide verified Elite-only romantic introductions for eligible singles who request them. Provide Elite couples with relevant opportunities to meet other couples and explore shared experiences. Respect relationship status, age requirements, consent and personal preferences. Allow Elite members to voluntarily choose broader eligible connections. Do not disclose financial verification details as part of relationship matching.

## 28. Elite Private Affairs

Allow Elite members to create sophisticated invitation-only conversations and gatherings. Provide: private meeting creation; public meeting creation; approval-required attendance; individual member invitations; secure external guest invitations; advanced scheduling options; host controls; participant management; meeting privacy settings; branded invitations; appropriate calendar integration.

Preserve the highest scheduling priority for pending Elite requests. Protect confirmed reservations and avoid unnecessary displacement of previously scheduled members.

## 29. Elite Discretion and Privacy

Provide enhanced privacy controls, including: profile visibility preferences; Elite-only discovery options; invitation preferences; availability visibility; online status controls; selective activity visibility; private meeting visibility; connection request restrictions; blocking and reporting.

Never allow membership status to override another member's consent, privacy or safety. Restrict human access to confidential communications and verification information. Preserve the existing Art of Connect Safety Shield.

## 30. Elite Member Service

Develop a dedicated Elite support and assistance experience. Provide the highest authorized priority for: membership assistance; technical support; scheduling assistance; private meeting support; account issues; feature navigation; service concerns.

Provide clear case tracking and escalation procedures. Design automated assistance to resolve routine requests. Use authorized human staff for issues requiring personal intervention. Present staffing requirements and proposed service response standards to the owner. Do not promise 24-hour human availability without approved resources.

## 31. Elite All-Inclusive Platform Privileges

Elite Concierge must include all authorized platform services. Do not require Elite members to continually purchase additional feature access. Do not impose additional platform speaking fees, private meeting fees or feature-unlocking charges for included services. The only additional platform charge should be Journey usage beyond the member's included allowance, consistent with the owner's approved pricing structure. Clearly distinguish independent third-party expenses from platform membership charges. Apply transparent, owner-approved technical capacity and fair-use controls without hidden fees.

## 32. Membership Benefit Progression

Maintain meaningful distinctions among membership levels.

One Society: applicable society benefits; personalized Journey access within allowance; deeper eligible connections; included speaking privileges; topic submission.

Two Societies: all applicable benefits of purchased societies; expanded experiences; topic submission and available scheduling selection.

Complete Society: access across all three societies; advanced hosting privileges; member moderation following authorization.

Executive Private: premium Executive experience; priority scheduling; private meeting privileges; professional collaboration; advanced privacy; executive support.

Elite Concierge: highest scheduling priority; verified Elite experiences; all included platform services; advanced introductions; exclusive communities; premium collaboration; highest-priority support; full authorized membership privileges.

Do not reduce previously approved benefits without owner authorization.

## 33. Membership Retention and Discovery

Help members understand and use the benefits they are paying for. Provide: membership welcome guidance; feature directory; What's New updates; relevant benefit suggestions; personalized event recommendations; optional weekly summaries; clear membership comparison information; appropriate upgrade previews.

Do not use deceptive upgrade prompts, artificial scarcity or manipulative engagement. Provide legitimate value rather than relying on constant notifications.

## 34. Owner Management and Analytics

Expand the Owner Portal to oversee: paid membership engagement; feature usage; membership entitlements; trial conversions; upgrade conversions; subscription retention; cancellation reasons; Journey usage; conversation attendance; connection activity; Executive service utilization; Elite service utilization; private meeting capacity; support requests; moderation expenses; AI expenses; infrastructure expenses; membership revenue; service profitability.

Use aggregated analytics where appropriate. Do not provide unrestricted access to private journals, counseling information or confidential communications. Give the owner appropriate controls over membership entitlements, feature availability, operating limits and approved pricing.

## 35. Technology and Cost Controls

Reuse existing platform infrastructure wherever possible. Maintain a central membership entitlement system. Enforce access permissions on the server and database, not merely through hidden or greyed-out interface elements.

Review: AI usage costs; live audio costs; video conferencing costs; storage expenses; translation expenses; human moderation expenses; support staffing; concurrent meeting capacity; premium service operating costs.

Present projected costs and implementation options before purchasing additional services. Prioritize scalable automation while preserving appropriate human oversight. Do not introduce expensive third-party services without owner approval.

## 36. Development Sequence

First, audit all existing paid, Executive and Elite functionality against this directive. Identify: already operational features; partially implemented features; missing capabilities; duplicate functionality; security or membership permission gaps; external infrastructure dependencies.

Then implement in coordinated phases:

Phase One: Membership feature directory, dashboard enhancements, entitlement verification and improved navigation.

Phase Two: Personalized member discovery, Connection Tables, Professional Society enhancements, singles, couples and paid growth experiences.

Phase Three: Complete Executive Private experience and approved private meeting capabilities.

Phase Four: Complete Elite Concierge dashboard, exclusive experiences, advanced introductions, Elite communities and premium service management.

Phase Five: Approved advanced conferencing, collaboration, accessibility and international communication features.

Coordinate these phases with the existing Becoming Circle development roadmap. Do not interrupt working development or duplicate completed functionality.

## 38. Commercial Launch Restrictions

Maintain all existing commercial launch restrictions. Do not activate: public commercial enrollment; live financial charges; unapproved paid infrastructure; unapproved clinical services; unapproved membership pricing changes; unapproved external service commitments. Maintain Stripe Sandbox. Obtain explicit owner authorization before commercial activation.

The experience must be intuitive.

The service must be exceptional.
