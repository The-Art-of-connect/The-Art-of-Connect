# 08 — SOCIAL ECOSYSTEM — COUPLES CONNECTION COMMUNITY

_Founder directive, kept word for word. Status: **designed — recorded in the Development Tracker and QA Center; not built**._
_The build blueprint is in [docs/social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md](../social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md)._

Please expand the Couples experience within our Social Ecosystem Master Blueprint.

I want couples to have an active social community where they can connect with other couples, develop meaningful friendships, travel together, share experiences and help one another.

Create a dedicated Couples Connection Community.

## Couples Networking

Allow couples to:

* Discover and connect with other couples.
* Find couples with similar interests, lifestyles and goals.
* Find local, nationwide and international connections.
* Create shared couple profiles and photograph galleries with mutual consent.
* Follow, communicate and participate in couples communities.
* Plan double dates, social gatherings and group activities.

## Couples Travel Exchange

Create a dedicated travel community where couples can:

* Post upcoming travel plans.
* Request destination information.
* Ask for hotel, restaurant and excursion recommendations.
* Share travel reviews and experiences.
* Upload travel photographs and videos.
* Share itineraries voluntarily.
* Discover other couples traveling to the same destination.
* Invite other couples to participate in trips.
* Create private group conversations for upcoming vacations.
* Coordinate activities through our existing calendar.

Allow Journey to assist eligible paid members with trip planning, itinerary suggestions and relevant recommendations.

## Couples Connection Circles

Create communities covering travel, newlyweds, entrepreneurial couples, empty nesters, couples over 50, date nights, marriage growth, local connections, Elite couples and shared adventures.

Allow eligible members to create additional communities under appropriate moderation and owner controls.

## Couple-to-Couple Communication

Allow couples to communicate through shared conversations, group messages, community discussions and event invitations.

Both partners must retain their individual accounts and privacy rights.

Shared couple profiles, messages, photographs and trip plans must use appropriate mutual consent and visibility controls.

Keep individual journals, private Journey conversations, counseling records and personal assessments confidential.

Do not automatically publish exact travel dates, home addresses or live locations.

Maintain a clear separation between couples friendship networking and singles romantic matchmaking.

## Integration

Connect this experience to our existing:

* Connection Feed.
* Connection Moments.
* Connection Circles.
* Connection Experiences.
* Couples calendars.
* Member profiles.
* Private messaging.
* Journey intelligence.
* Elite Concierge.

Add all requirements to the Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

Reuse existing architecture wherever possible and obtain my approval before introducing additional paid travel or booking integrations.

Maintain all commercial launch restrictions.

The goal is for couples to have meaningful friendships, shared adventures and a supportive community beyond their individual relationship.
