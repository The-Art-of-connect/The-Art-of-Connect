# 12 — FIND YOUR CIRCLE: OPEN, SPECIALIZED AND VERIFIED ELITE CIRCLES

_Founder directive, kept word for word. Status: **phase 1 in development** — see [docs/social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md](../social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md) §7._

Please expand Find Your Circle and the Connection Intelligence Engine to support multiple specialized communities within each primary interest category.

People may share the same interests but have different lifestyles, financial preferences, ambitions, experience levels and social expectations.

For example, Travel & Adventure should include Affordable Adventures, Luxury Travel, International Explorers, Cruise Enthusiasts, Solo Travelers and Elite Travel.

Business should include Aspiring Entrepreneurs, Established Business Owners, Growth & Expansion, Executive Leadership and Elite Business.

Apply this same structure to friendships, social activities, dining, singles, couples, personal development, retirement, hobbies and other community categories.

Create three types of communities:

* Open Circles.
* Specialized Interest and Lifestyle Circles.
* Verified Elite Circles.

Use authorized personality assessments, interests, lifestyles, goals and member preferences to recommend appropriate circles.

However, recommendations must never become mandatory assignments.

Allow members to:

* View recommended communities.
* Explore all eligible communities.
* Join multiple circles.
* Select their preferred experiences and lifestyle interests.
* Change their selections at any time.
* Discover communities outside their usual preferences.
* Participate in different events and meetings.
* Receive optional updates from each joined community.
* Leave communities whenever they choose.

Do not restrict ordinary lifestyle or luxury-interest groups based solely on income or professional status.

Only explicitly designated Elite communities require verified Elite membership.

Elite members should be able to participate in their exclusive circles and the broader community whenever they choose.

Allow members to distinguish between seeking mentorship, offering mentorship, meeting compatible peers and exploring new experiences.

Each circle must have its own community feed, member directory, events, announcements, discussions, photographs, videos, messaging and optional notifications.

Recommendations should consider both shared and complementary personality characteristics rather than matching people solely because they have similar incomes or professional accomplishments.

Give me owner controls to create, rename, manage and expand community categories and access requirements.

Integrate these requirements into the Social Ecosystem Master Blueprint, Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

The purpose is to give members meaningful choices, not place them into permanent social categories.

Help people find their people while allowing them to experience different people.

The system recommends. The member chooses.
