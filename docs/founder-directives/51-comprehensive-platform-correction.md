THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive 51 — Comprehensive Platform Correction, Luxury Experience and Functional Readiness

Founder’s Standard

Simplicity on the outside. Sophistication on the inside.

We do not need to be first. We need to be worth choosing.

The attached 145-address page-by-page audit establishes preliminary page accessibility. It does not establish complete functionality or production readiness.

Use the entire audit as the foundation for this corrective development phase. Review all existing implementations, founder directives, database structures, development branches and approved designs before creating additional features.

1. Complete Platform Inventory

Create an authoritative inventory of every platform route, feature, service, button, form, interactive element, database function and member workflow.

Include public pages, authenticated pages, dynamic pages, restricted pages, unlinked routes, Owner Portal pages and administrative functions.

Reconcile every approved founder directive against the actual implementation.

Identify features that are:

* Fully functional.
* Partially functional.
* Database-only.
* Interface-only.
* Disconnected from their intended destination.
* Duplicated.
* Awaiting configuration.
* Awaiting founder approval.
* Awaiting external providers.
* Not yet implemented.

A feature must not be marked complete simply because its database table exists, its page loads or its automated tests pass.

2. Luxury Design and Presentation

Preserve the approved brand identity, master logo, cream palette, charcoal, burgundy, champagne gold and signature orange accents.

Establish consistent platform-wide standards for:

* Typography and heading hierarchy.
* Photography and image quality.
* Buttons and interactive controls.
* Cards, icons and emblems.
* Form fields and descriptions.
* Margins, spacing and alignment.
* Pop-ups and notifications.
* Loading, success, error and empty states.
* Mobile, tablet and desktop layouts.
* Accessibility and contrast.

Remove unnecessary repetition, excessive text, confusing navigation and generic placeholder wording.

Every page must have an understandable purpose, a descriptive page title and a clear next action.

Do not introduce unapproved feature names or redesign the established brand without founder approval.

3. Home and Global Navigation

Maintain the approved primary navigation:

Home · Connect · Journey · My World

Keep one clear primary search experience on Home. A compact global search may remain accessible elsewhere without unnecessarily duplicating controls.

3. Home and Global Navigation — Continued

* Personalize Home according to membership, interests, relationship status, goals and saved progress.

4. Membership Experience and Revenue

* Apply the approved membership entitlements consistently across the entire platform.
* Recommend the lowest appropriate upgrade that unlocks the requested feature without removing existing benefits.
* Identify the best-value membership when supported by actual benefits and approved pricing.
* Display accurate membership prices, additional costs, trial eligibility and included benefits.
* Offer the complimentary 15-day experience where eligible.
* Free members must have the approved complimentary educational experience and the option to purchase Journey credits once authorized.
* Couples must retain their relationship experience when upgrading or adding business services.
* Elite Concierge includes all applicable platform services. Additional Journey usage is the only additional platform feature charge.
* Never charge members twice for an included benefit.
* Membership distinctions and identity verification must remain separate.

5. Journey Intelligence and Continuity

Journey must function as an intelligent platform navigator, personal development companion and authorized business assistant.

Implement and verify the approved monthly included AI spending allowances:

* Free: $0 complimentary personalized AI usage.
* Singles: $5 per member.
* Couples: $5 per person, $10 per couple.
* Professional: $10 per member.
* Dual Society: $10 per member.
* Executive Private: $15 per member.
* Elite Concierge: $25 per member.

These are company AI cost allowances, not membership prices.

Use actual OpenAI model costs to calculate usage. Do not invent voice-minute conversions or advertise unlimited usage.

Provide a consistent usage balance across Home, Journey, membership settings and the Owner Portal.

Additional purchased usage must follow the founder-approved credit pricing structure.

Journey must recognize previous conversations, completed lessons, milestones, goals and personal development progress when authorized by the member.

Respect privacy boundaries between personal, romantic and professional experiences.

7. In Love — Singles and Couples

Create an intuitive relationship experience with familiar matchmaking navigation while maintaining The Art of Connect’s original identity.

Singles must have a complete experience from onboarding through discovery, compatibility, mutual interest, messaging and The Exclusive Connection.

Couples must have a lifelong relationship development experience, including partner invitations, shared goals, private reflections, communication exercises, engagement, marriage, anniversaries and relationship milestones.

Support separation, divorce and returning to an individual experience without requiring a former partner’s approval.

Never automatically expose private reflections or reactivate Singles discovery following a separation.

Relationship changes must not automatically terminate independent business partnerships.
