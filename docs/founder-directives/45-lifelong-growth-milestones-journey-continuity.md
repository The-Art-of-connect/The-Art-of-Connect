THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — Lifelong Growth, Milestones and Journey Continuity

The Art of Connect must provide an ongoing, evolving member experience that documents personal, relationship and professional growth from the beginning of membership throughout the member’s lifetime.

1. My Personal Discovery Portfolio

Expand the existing My Personal Discovery Portfolio into a chronological record of the member’s growth.

Preserve original assessments, reflections, goals, milestones, accomplishments and subsequent updates.

Members must be able to compare their earlier perspectives with their present perspectives without overwriting their historical records.

Include meaningful growth experiences from In Life, In Love and In Business, subject to member consent and applicable privacy permissions.

Do not create unnecessary duplicate features or dashboards.

2. Journey Continuity

Journey must maintain authorized continuity across conversations and eligible experiences.

Journey should understand:

* Where the member began.
* Their personal, relationship and professional goals.
* Important experiences and life transitions.
* Previously identified patterns.
* Accomplishments and breakthroughs.
* Changes in priorities and perspectives.
* Current goals and unfinished activities.

Journey must distinguish member-reported facts, completed platform activities and AI-generated observations.

Journey must never invent accomplishments, diagnose members or assume personal growth without supporting information.

3. Monthly Personal Growth Reports

Provide eligible members with a personalized monthly growth report that includes:

* Where they began.
* Changes since their previous report.
* Important milestones and accomplishments.
* Completed lessons and activities.
* Progress toward personal, relationship and professional goals.
* Meaningful changes in their perspectives.
* Areas they may wish to explore further.
* A personalized reflection from Journey.
* Suggested next steps.

Use meaningful written analysis rather than arbitrary numerical emotional growth scores.

Save monthly reports within My Personal Discovery Portfolio and allow members to review previous reports.

4. Milestone Recognition

Recognize important member-approved milestones, including:

* Birthdays and anniversaries.
* Personal breakthroughs.
* Educational accomplishments.
* Relationship transitions.
* Career achievements.
* Business launches and expansion.
* Funding and project accomplishments.
* Completed personal goals.
* Membership anniversaries.

Allow members to create their own milestones and control whether recognition is private or shared.

5. Notifications and Engagement

Deliver monthly report notifications and milestone acknowledgments through the Message Center.

Allow eligible members to open their report, discuss their progress with Journey and navigate directly to recommended activities.

Respect notification preferences.

6. Membership Continuity

Membership upgrades must preserve all existing history, progress, milestones and benefits.

A downgrade or cancellation must not automatically erase historical growth records.

Free members retain their eligible self-guided progress and milestone history. Personalized AI-generated reports and conversations must follow approved membership and Journey credit rules.

7. Privacy and Member Control

Obtain appropriate consent for long-term AI personalization.

Allow members to review, correct, export and delete their information, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Private reflections, relationship information and confidential business records must never become visible to other members without explicit authorization.

Shared Couples activities must remain separate from individual private reflections.

8. Owner Portal

Provide controls for monthly report scheduling, eligibility, notification preferences, milestone categories, AI spending limits and delivery monitoring.

Audit existing portfolio, Journey memory, milestone and progress-tracking functionality before implementing additional infrastructure.

Keep the member-facing experience simple and consistent with the official Brand Terminology Registry.

9. Testing

Include longitudinal growth tracking, monthly reporting, historical preservation, membership changes, privacy, accurate milestone recognition and Journey continuity in the comprehensive independent audit.

Do not activate additional paid infrastructure or production services without explicit founder approval.

Founder principle: The Art of Connect should remember where members began, recognize where they are today and support who they are becoming.

## Amendment

Amend the Lifelong Growth, Milestones and Journey Continuity directive.

Personalized growth reports must not be restricted to a mandatory monthly schedule.

During enrollment, ask members how frequently they would like Journey to prepare their personalized growth reports.

Offer the following options:

* Monthly
* Quarterly
* Every six months
* Annually
* On demand only

Allow members to change, pause or resume their reporting preferences at any time through their account settings.

Journey must continue maintaining authorized historical continuity, tracking progress, accomplishments, important life experiences and milestones regardless of the selected reporting frequency.

Important milestone acknowledgments should remain separate from scheduled growth reports and respect individual notification preferences.

Save all generated growth reports chronologically within My Personal Discovery Portfolio.

Each report should compare the member’s original starting point, previous reflections, current progress and evolving goals.

Apply approved membership entitlements, privacy permissions and AI spending controls.

Record this amendment as part of the permanent founder directive.

Maintain all existing production activation restrictions.
