THE ART OF CONNECT, LLC

Founder Directive — Relationship Transitions and Flexible Business Participation

1. Flexible Business Participation

Members must be able to establish business projects individually or jointly.

Within CONNECT 360 and Business Deal Rooms, authorized members must be able to invite additional participants throughout the life of a business project.

Participants may include romantic partners, business partners, investors, accountants, attorneys and other professional advisers.

Provide appropriate invitations, acceptance requirements, participant permissions, responsibilities, removal procedures and audit histories.

Adding a participant must not automatically grant business ownership, access to unrelated confidential information or authority to bind other participants.

2. Relationship Status Management

The Couples experience must support real-life relationship transitions.

Allow members to update their relationship status through Settings, including:

* Committed relationship
* Engagement
* Marriage
* Separation
* Divorce in progress
* Divorced
* Relationship ended

Relationship status changes must not automatically cancel memberships or remove unrelated benefits.

3. Disconnecting the Couples Experience

Either partner must be able to independently disconnect from the shared Couples experience without requiring the other partner’s permission.

Provide a clear, respectful confirmation process explaining the effect on shared activities and information.

Immediately revoke future unauthorized access to shared relationship spaces.

Preserve individual accounts, private reflections, personal growth records, educational progress, Journey conversations and independent professional information.

Establish appropriate retention, export and privacy rules for previously shared records.

Include a confidential safety pathway for members who need to disconnect without exposing sensitive circumstances to their former partner.

4. Transition from Couples to Singles

Members who discontinue their Couples experience must have the option to return to individual experiences.

Do not automatically activate Singles matchmaking.

Allow members to choose when they are ready to participate, subject to existing age, relationship eligibility, identity verification and membership requirements.

Allow members to update their relationship preferences and personal discovery information without overwriting their historical progress.

5. Separation and Divorce Support

Journey must provide compassionate, personalized educational support during separation, divorce and relationship transitions.

Support self-discovery, emotional growth, healthy boundaries, confidence, personal independence, financial education and preparation for future relationships.

Provide appropriate referrals to licensed counselors, attorneys and financial professionals when necessary.

Journey must not pressure members to reconcile, remain in unsafe relationships or begin another relationship before they are ready.

6. Independent Business Continuity

A romantic relationship ending must not automatically terminate an existing business collaboration.

Business Deal Rooms, professional partnerships, shared business projects and financial arrangements must remain governed by their own authorized permissions and agreements.

Support discussions and documentation relating to business restructuring, buyouts, ownership transitions and dissolution, with appropriate professional review.

Never automatically change legal ownership, financial rights or binding obligations based on relationship status.

7. Lifelong Growth Continuity

Preserve the member’s authorized historical journey within My Personal Discovery Portfolio.

Journey should recognize relationship transitions as meaningful life events and adapt future guidance to the member’s current circumstances.

Respect individual reporting preferences, privacy settings and membership entitlements.

8. Testing and Permanent Enforcement

Include the following scenarios in the comprehensive customer experience audit:

* Individual business project followed by a partner invitation.
* Additional business partners joining an existing project.
* Participant permission changes and removal.
* Couples transitioning through engagement and marriage.
* Unilateral Couples disconnection.
* Separation and divorce.
* Safe disconnection under privacy-sensitive circumstances.
* Voluntary return to Singles.
* Former couples maintaining a business partnership.
* Preservation of individual records and membership benefits.
* Prevention of unauthorized access by former partners.

Audit existing functionality before creating duplicate features.

Maintain simplicity, consistent naming and all existing production activation restrictions.

Founder principle: Relationships may change, but personal growth, individual identity and professional opportunities must continue.
