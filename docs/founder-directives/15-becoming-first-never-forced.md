# 15 — THE ART OF BECOMING FIRST, NEVER FORCED

_Founder directive, November 26, 2026, kept word for word. Status: **entry choices built** (free self-discovery, 15-day trial or membership now; no required order). Recommendation use of discovery information: see directive 16._

Peace,

The Art of Becoming is our complimentary foundation for every individual.

I strongly encourage members to learn who they are before seeking friendships, romantic relationships, business partnerships or other meaningful connections.

However, completing The Art of Becoming must not be a mandatory prerequisite for purchasing a membership.

Allow individuals to choose between:

* Beginning their complimentary self-discovery journey.
* Purchasing an eligible membership immediately.
* Beginning an applicable 15-day membership trial.

Encourage self-discovery throughout their membership experience, even if they choose to purchase a membership first.

Use completed and authorized self-discovery information to improve community recommendations and connection compatibility.

When information is incomplete, explain that completing additional discovery activities may improve the relevance of recommendations.

The Art of Becoming remains free, including The Art of Self, self-guided educational lessons, personal discovery, identity, self-love, emotional intelligence, boundaries, love languages, private journaling and the Discovery Portfolio.

Interactive social experiences, communities, friendships, events, business networking, singles, couples and personalized Journey intelligence require the applicable paid membership or authorized trial.

Maintain private self-discovery information and never expose individual assessment answers to other members.

Our philosophy is to encourage people to understand themselves first, without forcing them to follow a particular sequence.

Discover yourself. Find your people. Experience life together.
