# 09 — SOCIAL ECOSYSTEM — THE CONNECT CALENDAR

_Founder directive, kept word for word. Status: **designed — recorded in the Development Tracker and QA Center; not built**._
_The build blueprint is in [docs/social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md](../social-ecosystem/master-blueprint.md)._

Add a comprehensive community-driven Events and Experiences Center to our Social Ecosystem Master Blueprint.

Call it The Connect Calendar.

I want members to be able to inform other members about social, professional, educational, travel and lifestyle events.

Members do not have to be the event organizer to share an event they discover.

## Event Sharing and Creation

Allow members to create original events or share existing events with:

* Event name and description.
* Event photograph or promotional flyer.
* Event category.
* Date, time and time zone.
* City, state and country.
* Virtual or in-person designation.
* Venue information.
* Admission cost, if applicable.
* Official event website or registration link.
* Organizer information.
* Applicable membership eligibility.
* Event visibility and privacy settings.

Clearly distinguish member-organized events from externally organized events shared by members.

## Member Interaction

Allow eligible members to:

* Mark themselves interested.
* Mark themselves attending.
* Request additional information.
* Find other members to attend with.
* Invite connections.
* Participate in event discussions.
* Create private event group conversations.
* Save and share events.
* Add events to their personal or shared couples calendars.
* Receive optional event reminders.
* Share photographs, videos and experiences after attending.

## Event Categories

Include business networking, conferences, social gatherings, singles events, couples events, travel, cruises, concerts, festivals, education, webinars, wellness, community service and Elite experiences.

## Event Discovery

Allow members to discover events locally, nationwide, internationally and virtually.

Provide filters for location, date, interests, event category, price and applicable membership eligibility.

Allow Journey to recommend relevant events to authorized paid members based on their preferences and interests.

## Safety and Authenticity

Implement event reporting, moderation, organizer verification where appropriate, fraud prevention and privacy protections.

Do not automatically expose member locations or attendance without consent.

Distinguish verified organizers from ordinary members sharing public events.

Do not allow misleading event listings, fraudulent ticket sales or unauthorized payment collection.

## Integration

Integrate events with our social feed, profiles, communities, messaging, calendars, notifications, webinars, couples networking, singles experiences and Elite Concierge.

Allow free members to discover and share appropriate events within their authorized experiences.

Add this initiative to the Development Tracker and Quality Assurance Center.

Reuse existing infrastructure wherever possible. Do not introduce additional paid ticketing or event-booking services without my approval.

Our purpose is to encourage members to experience life together, not simply interact online.
