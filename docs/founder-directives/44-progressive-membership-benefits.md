# 44 — PROGRESSIVE MEMBERSHIP BENEFITS

_Founder Directive, November 28, 2026, kept word for word. Status: **permanent founder directive and standing development requirement.**_

Founder Directive — Progressive Membership Benefits

Every membership level up within The Art of Connect must provide additional value without removing existing membership benefits.

Members must only gain when they level up. They must never lose.

Mandatory Membership Rules

* Preserve all existing membership benefits when a member upgrades.
* Unlock additional features, experiences, services and applicable allowances.
* Preserve existing profiles, connections, messages, educational progress, saved research, business projects and personal information.
* Never reduce existing Journey allowances as a result of an upgrade.
* Never require members to repurchase functionality already included in their membership.
* Never recommend a membership as a level up if it removes any existing entitlement.
* Where memberships cover different experiences, recommend an appropriate combined membership that preserves the member’s existing benefits.

Membership Recommendations

Clearly communicate:

* What the member already receives.
* What additional benefits they will gain.
* The additional membership cost.
* Which eligible membership offers the best overall value.

Use positive language such as “Everything you enjoy today, plus more.”

Elite Concierge

Elite Concierge represents the highest membership experience and includes all applicable platform services across In Life, In Love and In Business.

The only additional platform charge for Elite Members is additional Journey usage beyond their included allowance.

Permanent Enforcement

Apply this requirement to membership pricing, upgrade pop-ups, checkout, Journey recommendations, database permissions and all membership entitlement logic.

Test every possible membership upgrade combination to ensure no existing benefits are lost.

Record this as a permanent founder directive and standing development requirement.

Maintain all existing production activation restrictions.

Founder principle: Every level up means more access, more opportunities and greater value — never less.
